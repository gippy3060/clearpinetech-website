How to Move Everything to a New iPhone

A new iPhone is exciting, until you wonder whether your photos, contacts and messages will come across. They can, and Apple builds in a way to do it. This guide explains the steps in plain words. Take your time. Keep both phones plugged in or well charged, and set aside about an hour.

Before you start

  1. Know your Apple ID and password. This is the email address and password you use for the App Store or iCloud. If you are unsure, check on your old phone under Settings, tapping your name at the top.
  2. Update the old iPhone in Settings, General, Software Update, if it offers an update.
  3. Connect both phones to your home Wi-Fi.
  4. Make sure you have a recent backup of the old phone (see the next section). A backup is a safety copy.

Choose a way to move your things

Option 1: Phone to phone (Quick Start)

This is usually the simplest way. Turn on the new iPhone and put it next to the old one. The old phone shows a message offering to set up the new one. Follow the prompts, point the old phone's camera at the pattern on the new one, and enter your passcode when asked. When it asks how to transfer, choose to move the data directly from the old iPhone. Keep the phones close together until it says it has finished.

Option 2: From an iCloud backup

On the old iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap iCloud, then iCloud Backup, and tap Back Up Now. Stay on Wi-Fi until it finishes. On the new iPhone, choose to restore from an iCloud backup during setup and sign in with your Apple ID. iCloud gives you 5 GB of free space, which may not be enough for a phone full of photos. If it is not enough, Option 1 does not use iCloud space.

Moving from an Android phone

During setup of the new iPhone, there is a choice to move data from Android. Apple has a free app for Android phones called Move to iOS that carries across contacts, photos and videos, calendars and email accounts. Some things, such as apps you bought, may need to be downloaded again from the App Store, and some paid apps need to be bought again.

After the move

  • Photos: open the Photos app and check that your recent pictures are there. Photos can keep loading for a while after setup.
  • Apps: apps may keep downloading in the background. You may need to sign in again to some of them, such as banking or email.
  • Messages and contacts: check that your contacts are there and that a test text works.
  • Two-factor codes and bank apps: some need to be set up again on the new phone. Do this before you give away or trade in the old one.
  • Wipe the old phone last, only when you are sure everything is on the new one.

Common snags

  • "Not enough iCloud storage": use Option 1, or pay for more iCloud space if you want to keep backing up to it.
  • Transfer takes a long time: that is normal for lots of photos. Keep both phones plugged in and on Wi-Fi.
  • Forgotten Apple ID password: only Apple can reset it. We cannot reset or unlock accounts for you, but we can walk you through Apple's own steps.
  • Phone number not moving: moving your number to a new phone is done with your phone company, not in the iPhone settings.
