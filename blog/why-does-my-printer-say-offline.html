Why Does My Printer Say Offline? Simple Fixes

The printer is switched on. The lights look fine. But your computer says "Printer offline" and nothing prints. It is one of the most common home computer problems, and it is usually easy to fix.

"Offline" almost never means the printer is broken. It means your computer cannot reach the printer right now. Try the steps below in order. Go slowly. You cannot harm anything by trying them.

Step 1: Switch everything off and on again

  1. Turn the printer off. Wait 30 seconds, then turn it on.
  2. Restart your computer.
  3. If the printer connects by Wi-Fi, unplug your Wi-Fi router (the box from your internet company) for 30 seconds, then plug it back in. Wait a few minutes for the lights to settle.

This fixes the problem more often than any other step.

Step 2: Check the printer is on your Wi-Fi

  • Look at the printer's small screen or its Wi-Fi light. Does it show it is connected?
  • Your computer and printer must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Many routers have a second "guest" network, and a printer on one network cannot be seen from the other.
  • If you recently changed your Wi-Fi name or password, or got a new router, the printer has probably lost its connection and needs to be joined again. Printers differ, so check its screen menu or manual for "Wi-Fi setup".

Step 3: On Windows, check the "offline" setting

  1. Open Settings, then Bluetooth & devices (Windows 11) or Devices (Windows 10), then Printers & scanners.
  2. Click your printer, then Open print queue.
  3. Click Printer in the menu at the top. If Use Printer Offline has a tick, click it to remove the tick.
  4. Cancel any old stuck jobs in the list, then try printing again.

Step 4: On a Mac, remove and add the printer

  1. Open the Apple menu, then System Settings (or System Preferences on older Macs), then Printers & Scanners.
  2. Select your printer and remove it with the minus (–) button.
  3. Click the plus (+) button, choose your printer from the list and add it again.

Step 5: Remove and re-add the printer on Windows

If it still says offline, go back to Printers & scanners, remove the printer, then choose Add device and let Windows find it again. This often clears out an old, broken connection.

Why does it keep going offline?

  • The printer's network address changed. Home routers hand out addresses that can change after a power cut or restart. Your computer may still be looking in the old place.
  • The printer goes to sleep and drops off the Wi-Fi to save power.
  • The Wi-Fi signal is weak where the printer sits. A printer far from the router may fall off. Moving it closer is a simple test.
  • An old or damaged driver (the small program that lets the computer talk to the printer).
  • A router or Wi-Fi change that the printer was never told about.
