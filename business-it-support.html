Business IT Support for Small and Mid-Sized Offices in the Lower Mainland & Fraser Valley

Managed IT · Helpdesk · Security · Microsoft 365

If you run a small business, an office, a clinic or a law practice, you probably don’t need an IT department. You need someone to answer when email stops working, keep laptops patched, make sure the backups actually restore, and tell you plainly what is worth spending money on. That is what business IT support from Clearpine is.

We are a managed service provider based in Abbotsford, supporting offices across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver. Most support is delivered remotely, so it works the same whether you are in Abbotsford, Surrey or Vancouver, with on-site visits when something needs hands on the hardware.

What small business IT support includes

  • Helpdesk — your staff call or email a real person when something breaks, instead of searching forums or waiting on a friend who “knows computers.”
  • Device and patch management — laptops, desktops and servers kept updated and monitored so small issues are caught before they stop work.
  • Microsoft 365 administration — accounts, email, Teams, SharePoint, shared mailboxes, onboarding and offboarding.
  • Cybersecurity basics done properly — multi-factor authentication, firewall configuration, network segmentation and tested backups, scoped to what a small business actually needs.
  • Backups you can restore — not just “a backup is running,” but a copy that has been test-restored. See why your backups probably aren’t working.
  • Network, Wi-Fi and hardware — new office setups, relocations, structured cabling and equipment refreshes through our hardware deployment service.
  • Co-managed IT — if you already have an IT person, we back them up with after-hours cover, project help and holiday continuity. See co-managed IT.

Managed IT, break-fix or in-house?

There are three common ways a small business handles IT, and each fits a different situation:

  • Break-fix — you pay when something breaks. It looks cheap until a bad month, and nobody is responsible for prevention, patching or backups in between.
  • Managed IT — a predictable monthly fee per user, with ongoing monitoring, support and security. This is the usual fit for offices of roughly 5 to 50 people.
  • In-house IT — makes sense once you have enough people and complexity to keep someone busy full time, often alongside an outside partner. See managed IT vs in-house for small business.

How to choose an IT support provider

Whoever you talk to, ask these questions and get the answers in writing:

  • What exactly is included in the monthly fee, and what is billed extra?
  • How long is the agreement, and what happens if it isn’t working out?
  • Who will actually answer the phone, and will you deal with the same people?
  • How are backups tested, and when did you last see a restore?
  • What happens when an employee joins or leaves?
  • Do they price on-site visits clearly?
