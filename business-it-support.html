Most small businesses do not need an IT department; they need someone who keeps email, laptops, Wi-Fi, backups and security working, and who answers when something breaks. Clearpine Technologies is a managed IT provider based in Abbotsford, BC. We support businesses remotely and on site across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland.

What business IT support includes

  • Help desk for your staff. Remote support for users and devices during business hours, so people are not troubleshooting on their own.
  • Microsoft 365 administration. Accounts, email, MFA, licences and sharing permissions set up and kept tidy. Licences are billed separately.
  • Security basics done properly. Patching, endpoint protection, multi-factor authentication and ransomware readiness. See our cybersecurity services.
  • Backups that restore. Backups tested rather than assumed. Read why your backups probably are not working.
  • Network, firewall and Wi-Fi. Equipment documented, updated and monitored.
  • New hires, departures and hardware. Laptops provisioned, access granted and, just as important, removed. See hardware deployment.
  • On-site visits when needed. On-site visits are billed separately, but free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely. See on-site IT support costs in the Fraser Valley.

What it costs

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement, delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately. On-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely.

Business IT support: common questions

What is business IT support?

Business IT support is ongoing help for the computers, accounts, network, email, backups and security your staff rely on. With managed IT support, a provider monitors and maintains these systems and helps your team when something breaks, instead of you paying only when there is a crisis.

How much does small business IT support cost?

Our managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. It is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, although on-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely.

Do you support small businesses in Surrey, Langley, Vancouver and the rest of the Lower Mainland?

Yes. We are based in Abbotsford and support businesses remotely across Canada, with on-site visits in the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver. We have no offices outside Abbotsford.

Is managed IT better than hiring someone in-house?

It depends on your size and needs. Many small businesses find a managed provider more practical than one employee who is also a single point of failure. We compare the options honestly in our guide to managed IT versus in-house IT.

Can you do an IT assessment before we commit?

Yes. Our free IT assessment is no obligation and can be done on site at any location within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely. You get a written list of risks ranked by importance.
