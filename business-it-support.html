Most small businesses are too big to rely on a nephew who is good with computers and too small for a full-time IT hire. What business IT support covers

  • Help desk. Phone and email support for your staff, with defined response targets.
  • Devices and patching. Standard builds, updates on a schedule, and a current inventory of hardware and licences.
  • Microsoft 365. User setup and offboarding, mailbox management, multi-factor authentication and security hardening.
  • Backup you can restore from. Backups that are tested, not just scheduled. See why backups quietly fail.
  • Cybersecurity basics. Email filtering, endpoint protection, access controls and a plan for who to call if something goes wrong.
  • Network and Wi-Fi. Firewall, switching and wireless that do not fall over on a busy Monday.
  • Projects. Office moves, new-hire setups, hardware refreshes and cloud migrations, scoped in writing first.

Full detail is on our managed IT services page, and we can also co-manage alongside an existing IT person.

What it costs

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement, delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately. On-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely. Fewer than 5 users? We can set up custom billing.

How to choose an MSP

Every provider calls itself the best, so judge on specifics. Before you sign with anyone, including us, ask:

  • What is the price per user, in writing, and what is billed on top?
  • How fast do you respond, and who actually picks up the phone?
  • How do you test backups, and when did you last restore one for a client?
  • What are the term length and exit terms, and do I own my data and admin credentials?
  • Will you document my environment so I am never locked in?

Business IT support: common questions

What is managed IT support for a small business?

Managed IT support means a provider looks after your computers, Microsoft 365, backups, security updates and help desk for a predictable monthly fee, instead of you paying by the hour when something breaks. The aim is fewer problems, faster fixes and no surprise bills.

How much does small business IT support cost in the Lower Mainland?

Clearpine's managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement, delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, although on-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely. For businesses with fewer than 5 users, we can set up custom billing.

Do you provide on-site IT support in Surrey, Langley, Vancouver and the Fraser Valley?

Yes. From our Abbotsford head office we attend on site across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland, and provide remote support across Canada.

Can you work alongside our existing IT person or provider?

Yes. Co-managed IT adds help desk overflow, after-hours cover or project capacity to an in-house person, without replacing them.

What is a free IT assessment?

A free, no-obligation review of how your office's IT is set up: devices, Microsoft 365, backups, security and network. It can be done on site at any location within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely, and you get a written list of what we would change.

How do I choose the best MSP for my business?

Ask for the price in writing, what is and is not included, how quickly they respond, who actually answers the phone, how backups are tested and what happens if you leave. Our guide on choosing between an MSP and an independent contractor covers the trade-offs.
