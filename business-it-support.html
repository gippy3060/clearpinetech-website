Business IT Support & Managed IT for Lower Mainland Small Businesses

Small business IT support · Fraser Valley & Metro Vancouver

Most small and mid-sized businesses don’t need a full-time IT department. They need someone dependable to answer when a laptop, printer or mailbox stops working, and someone diligent enough that it happens less often. Clearpine provides that as managed IT support for offices, clinics and professional practices across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver, from our Abbotsford headquarters.

What business IT support from Clearpine includes

  • Helpdesk — staff reach a real person by phone or email, under a written service agreement with defined response times.
  • Devices and Microsoft 365 — standardised workstations, patching on a schedule, user setup and removal, licensing and mailbox administration.
  • Backups and monitoring — backups that are tested, not just scheduled, plus alerts that surface problems before staff notice.
  • Security basics done properly — multi-factor authentication, firewall configuration, access control and endpoint protection.
  • Projects — new offices, moves and hardware refreshes, including hardware deployment.

The full list is on our managed IT support and cybersecurity service pages.

How to compare managed service providers

Every MSP in the Lower Mainland says it is the best, so the claim tells you little. These questions tell you more, and we are happy to answer them for ourselves:

  1. Is the service agreement written, with response times you can hold the provider to?
  2. How are backups tested, and when was the last restore performed?
  3. Is multi-factor authentication enforced for every user, including administrators?
  4. What is billed on top of the monthly fee: licences, after-hours, on-site visits, projects?
  5. Who actually turns up on site, and how far away are they?
  6. What does it take to leave, and do you keep your own admin credentials and documentation?

If you are weighing options, our guides on managed IT versus in-house and an independent contractor versus an MSP set out the trade-offs.

Business IT support FAQ

What does business IT support include?

Helpdesk by phone and email, device and Microsoft 365 administration, patching, backup checks and monitoring, under a written agreement. Managed IT support is $95 per user per month (minimum 5 users, 1-year agreement), delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately; on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely.

Do you support small businesses in the Lower Mainland?

Yes. Clearpine is headquartered in Abbotsford and supports small and mid-sized businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver, remotely day to day and on site when needed.

How do I choose the best managed service provider (MSP)?

Ask for the agreement in writing, defined response times, how backups are tested, how multi-factor authentication is enforced, what is billed extra and how you exit. Then compare providers on those answers rather than on who claims to be best.

Can you work alongside our in-house IT person?

Yes. Co-managed IT gives an existing IT person after-hours cover, project help and holiday continuity without replacing them.
