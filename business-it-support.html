Business IT Support for Small and Mid-Sized Companies in the Lower Mainland

Fraser Valley · Metro Vancouver · Canada-wide

Clearpine is an Abbotsford managed service provider (MSP) that gives small and mid-sized businesses a proper IT department without the cost of hiring one: a helpdesk that answers, devices that are managed, security that is tested, and a written agreement that says what you get.

What business IT support includes

  • Helpdesk — your staff get a person to call or email when something breaks, with defined response times.
  • Device and Microsoft 365 management — patching, onboarding and offboarding, licensing and user administration.
  • Cybersecurity — multi-factor authentication, firewall configuration, endpoint protection and backups that are actually restore-tested.
  • Network and hardware — Wi-Fi, switching, servers, workstations and structured cabling for new offices, moves and refreshes.
  • Co-managed IT — a second set of hands and after-hours coverage for companies that already have an IT person.

Details for each are on the services page.

Small business IT support pricing

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Support is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, but on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. For offices with fewer than 5 users we offer custom billing on the same structure.

Break-fix, in-house or managed?

Calling someone only when things break leaves security and backups unattended until the day they matter. Hiring in-house is hard to justify for a small team and leaves you exposed during holidays and sick days. A managed plan sits between the two. See our comparisons: managed IT vs in-house IT for small business and independent contractor vs MSP.

Business IT support FAQ

What is included in business IT support?

Helpdesk for your staff, device and Microsoft 365 administration, proactive monitoring, security controls such as multi-factor authentication and tested backups, and hardware and network support, under a written service agreement.

How much does small business IT support cost in the Lower Mainland?

$95 per user per month (minimum 5 users, 1-year agreement), delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately; on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. Offices with fewer than 5 users get custom billing on the same structure.

Where do you provide business IT support?

Clearpine is headquartered in Abbotsford and supports businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver remotely, with on-site visits as needed. We also work Canada-wide.

Can we get a free IT assessment first?

Yes. The free, no-obligation assessment can be done on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford, for businesses of any size, or remotely.

Can you work alongside our in-house IT person?

Yes. Co-managed IT adds after-hours coverage, project help and continuity when your IT person is away.
