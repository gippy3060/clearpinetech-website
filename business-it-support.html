Most small businesses in the Lower Mainland do not need a full-time IT person. They need someone reliable to call when something breaks, and someone careful enough that things break less often. Usually IT ends up with whoever in the office is "good with computers", on top of their real job, until a laptop dies, an email account gets taken over or the backup turns out to have stopped months ago.

Clearpine Technologies is a small managed service provider (MSP) based in Abbotsford. We look after businesses across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland under a written service agreement, so what you get is spelled out, not hoped for. You deal with senior people who do the work themselves; see how we operate.

What our small business IT support includes

  • Helpdesk by phone and email. Your staff contact us directly, with defined response and resolution targets in the agreement that we report against monthly.
  • Computer and device management. Standard setups for new laptops and desktops, updates and patches on a schedule, endpoint protection, and the ability to lock or wipe a lost device.
  • Microsoft 365 administration. New and departing staff, licences, mailboxes, shared files and security settings such as multi-factor authentication. Licences themselves are billed separately at their cost to you.
  • Backups that are tested. We check that backups ran and test restores, because most failed recoveries are discovered during the recovery.
  • Proactive monitoring. Alerts for failing disks, full storage, stopped services and missed backups, so problems surface before your staff notice.
  • Asset and licence list. Kept current, so warranty expiries, renewals and end-of-life hardware do not ambush your budget.
  • Documentation you keep. Admin passwords, network details and settings are written down and belong to you. If you ever leave, another provider can pick up where we left off.

Need more? We also do cybersecurity work, hardware deployment, office moves and IT projects. If you already have an in-house IT person, co-managed IT gives them backup rather than replacing them.

Business IT services we cover

  • IT help desk and remote IT support: staff call or email us and we connect remotely to fix most problems the same way we would on site.
  • On-site IT support: when a problem needs hands on the hardware, we come to your office. On-site visits are billed separately, but are free if we cannot fix the problem remotely.
  • Computer and laptop support: slow or failing computers, warranty claims with the manufacturer, and setting up replacement laptops and desktops ready to work.
  • Network, Wi-Fi and firewall support: office Wi-Fi that reaches every room, separate guest Wi-Fi, firewall setup and updates, and internet outages chased with your provider.
  • Email and Microsoft 365 support: mailboxes, shared calendars, Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint, licences, multi-factor authentication and migrations from another email provider. See Microsoft 365 support.
  • Business phone systems (VoIP): choosing a VoIP provider, number porting and setup, supported by the same team that looks after your network.
  • Cybersecurity for small business: endpoint protection, patching, multi-factor authentication, phishing awareness and security reviews. See cybersecurity services.
  • Data backup and recovery: backups of computers, servers and Microsoft 365, with test restores so you know they work.
  • New staff setup and offboarding: accounts, laptop and access ready for day one, and access removed on the last day.
  • Printers, scanners and office equipment: network printers, scan-to-email and shared devices.
  • New office setups and office moves: network, Wi-Fi, workstations and printers planned and installed. See IT project management.
  • IT consulting and planning: hardware replacement schedules, licence reviews and an honest view of what to fix first.

Choosing a provider

How to choose the best MSP in the Lower Mainland

There is no single "best MSP" in the Lower Mainland. There is the provider whose agreement, price and way of working fit your business. Whoever you talk to, including us, ask these questions and get the answers in writing:

  1. What exactly does the per-user price include? Helpdesk, patching, monitoring, backup, security tools and Microsoft 365 administration, or only some of them?
  2. What are the response times, and are they in the contract? "We're usually quick" is not a response time.
  3. Who answers when we call? A named technician who knows your setup, or a ticket queue?
  4. How are on-site visits and after-hours work billed? And how far are they from your office? Travel time is real in the Fraser Valley.
  5. How do you test backups? Ask when they last did a test restore for a client your size.
  6. What happens if we leave? You should get all admin passwords, documentation and your data without a fight or an exit fee you did not know about.
  7. How long is the agreement, and what are the exit terms? Ours is a 1-year agreement; know the terms of any contract before you sign.
  8. Can we talk to the person who will do the work? At a small MSP, the answer should be yes.

Our free IT assessment is a low-risk way to judge us against that list: we look at your current setup and give you a written list of risks ranked by importance, whether or not you hire us.
