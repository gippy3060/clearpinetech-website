What business IT support from Clearpine includes

Most small businesses don't need a full-time IT person. They need someone who picks up when something breaks, and who does the routine work that stops things breaking in the first place. Our managed IT support covers both, under a written service agreement.

  • Help desk by phone and email. Staff call or email us when a computer, printer, mailbox or app stops working. You talk to the people who actually look after your systems.
  • Microsoft 365 administration. New staff set up, departing staff removed the same day, licences tidied, mailboxes and Teams managed, and sign-in security turned on properly.
  • Patching and device management. Windows, Mac and key applications updated on a schedule, with standard builds so a new laptop works the way the old one did.
  • Security basics done properly. Multi-factor authentication, managed endpoint protection, firewall settings that aren't factory defaults, and admin accounts kept separate from everyday ones. More on our cybersecurity work.
  • Backups that are tested. We restore files to prove the backups work, because a failed backup usually only comes to light when you need it. See why backups quietly fail.
  • Monitoring. Alerts for full disks, failing hardware, stopped services and missed backups, so we can often fix a problem before anyone notices it.
  • Asset and licence inventory. A current list of your devices, warranties and subscriptions, so renewals and end-of-life hardware can go in the budget ahead of time.
  • On-site help when it's needed. On-site visits are billed separately, but they're free when we can't fix the problem remotely.

How to choose the best MSP in the Lower Mainland

Search for "best MSP" or "best IT company near me" and you'll find dozens of providers from Vancouver to Chilliwack, all saying similar things. The best one for you is the one that gives clear answers to these questions, in writing:

  1. What exactly does the monthly fee cover? Get a list: help desk hours, patching, backups, security tools, Microsoft 365 admin, on-site visits. Compare like with like.
  2. What is billed on top? After-hours work, on-site travel, projects, new-computer setup and licences are the usual extras.
  3. Who answers the phone? A large help desk or a small team who know your office. Both can work, but you should know which you're getting.
  4. How far away are they? If a firewall or network switch fails, can someone reach your office the same day? A provider in Abbotsford and one in downtown Vancouver will give you different answers for Chilliwack or North Vancouver.
  5. How do they prove backups work? Ask when they last did a test restore for a client and what they checked.
  6. Do you get your documentation? Admin passwords, network diagrams and device lists should belong to you, so you can change providers without being held hostage.
  7. What does leaving look like? Contract length, notice period and any offboarding fees.

Our answers: the inclusions are listed above, extras are listed in the pricing section, you deal with senior people who do the work themselves, we're based in Abbotsford, and the documentation is yours. We'd rather you ask these questions of every provider you're considering.

What's the difference between business IT support and managed IT services?

People use both terms for the same thing. "Managed IT services" usually means a provider looks after your systems for a monthly fee, including prevention work. "IT support" can also mean paying by the hour when something breaks. Our business IT support is a managed service.

Do you only work with clinics and law firms?

No. We have dedicated pages for healthcare and legal practices because they have extra privacy obligations, but we support offices of all kinds: professional services, trades and construction offices, non-profits, retail back offices and more.

Can you take over from our current IT provider?

Yes. We'll start with a free assessment so you know where things stand, then work out the handover of admin access, documentation and any running contracts.

We already have an IT person. Can you help?

Yes, through co-managed IT. Your IT person stays in charge and we cover after hours, holidays, security tooling and extra project work.
