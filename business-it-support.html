Choosing a provider

How to choose the best MSP in the Lower Mainland for your business

There's no single best managed service provider for every business. Directory rankings and "top 10" lists are a starting point, but the right provider is the one whose service matches the size of your office, the hours you work and the information you hold. Ask every provider you're considering these eight questions, including us.

  1. What exactly is included in the monthly price? Get it in writing. Check whether security tools, backup storage, Microsoft 365 administration and on-site visits are included or charged as extras.
  2. What are your response times, and are they in the contract? "Best effort" isn't a commitment. Ask how response and resolution are reported to you.
  3. Who will answer when we call? Find out whether support is delivered locally, from elsewhere in Canada or offshore, and whether you'll deal with the same few people over time.
  4. How fast can someone be on site, and what does a visit cost? Remote support handles most problems, but a failed switch or a new printer still needs hands. Distance from the provider's office matters in the Fraser Valley.
  5. What's the contract length, and how do we leave? Ask what happens to your admin passwords, documentation and data at the end. You should own all of it.
  6. How do you test backups? The right answer involves regular test restores, not just a green tick in a dashboard.
  7. How do you handle privacy obligations? If you hold personal information, as clinics, law firms, accountants and most professional offices do, ask how the provider supports your duties under BC's PIPA and where your data is stored.
  8. Can you give us references from businesses like ours? A provider that mostly serves 200-person companies may not be a fit for a 10-person office, and the reverse is also true.

Where Clearpine fits: we're a small MSP, and we say so. You get a senior person on the phone, a published price, written scope before any work starts, and full documentation that belongs to you. We aren't the right choice if you need 24/7 coverage included in the base price, or if you're looking for a large provider with a big local staff. Questions

Business IT support: common questions

How much does small business IT support cost in the Lower Mainland?

Our managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users on a 1-year agreement. That covers remote help desk support during business hours, device management and patching, Microsoft 365 administration, backup verification, monitoring and asset tracking. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, and on-site visits are free if we can’t fix the problem remotely. Other Lower Mainland providers publish prices from roughly $80 to over $180 per user per month, so compare what each price includes rather than the headline number.

Do you support businesses with fewer than 5 employees?

Yes. The standard plan has a 5-user minimum, but for smaller offices we set up custom billing on the same per-user structure. Call (236) 258-3060 and we’ll work out what fits.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Abbotsford and support businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver, including Chilliwack, Mission, Langley, Surrey, Maple Ridge, Burnaby, New Westminster, Vancouver and North Vancouver. Day-to-day support is remote, and we come on site when a problem needs hands on equipment. Free IT assessments can be done on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford.

What's the difference between business IT support and managed IT services?

In practice they describe the same thing for most small businesses: an outside team that looks after your computers, email, network, security and backups for a monthly fee. "Managed" means the work is ongoing and proactive, with patching, monitoring and backup checks happening on a schedule, rather than someone you call only when something breaks.

Is 24/7 support included?

No. Our managed plan covers business hours remotely, and after-hours support is billed separately. We say so up front because many offices don’t need round-the-clock coverage, and those that do should know exactly what it costs before they sign.

We already have an IT person. Can you still help?

Yes. Co-managed IT is for businesses with an in-house IT person or team who need backup rather than a replacement: after-hours and vacation coverage, extra hands for projects, and security tooling, with responsibilities agreed in writing.

How do we switch from our current IT provider?

Start with a free IT assessment so we can see what you have. If you decide to move, we plan the handover with you: collect admin access and documentation, confirm backups are working before anything changes, and move services over in stages so staff aren’t left without email or files. If your current provider is doing a good job, we’ll tell you that too.
