Business & Small Business IT Support in the Lower Mainland

Managed IT for Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver offices

If you run a small or mid-sized business in the Lower Mainland, you probably don’t need an IT department. You need someone who answers when email stops working, keeps laptops patched, checks that backups restore, and tells you plainly what to spend money on. That is what Clearpine does, as a managed service provider (MSP) based in Abbotsford.

We are a smaller firm than the big Vancouver MSPs, and we would rather you judge us on a free assessment and a clear price than on a claim to be the biggest or the best.

What business IT support includes

  • Help desk and remote support — staff contact us when something breaks; we fix it remotely wherever possible.
  • Microsoft 365 administration — users, mailboxes, licences, shared drives, sign-in security.
  • Security basics done properly — multi-factor authentication, patching, endpoint protection, firewall configuration.
  • Backups that are tested — monitored, and restored on a schedule so you know they work.
  • Network and Wi-Fi — office networks, firewalls, access points and cabling.
  • New staff and equipment — onboarding, offboarding and hardware deployment.
  • Co-managed IT — support for a business that already has one IT person and needs backup.

Details of each are on the services page.

Small business IT support pricing

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Support is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, but on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. For offices with fewer than 5 users we offer custom billing on the same structure.

Break-fix or managed IT?

Break-fix means you call, we fix, you pay for the time. It suits very small setups, but nobody is watching for problems between calls. Managed IT is a flat monthly fee for proactive care: monitoring, patching, security and support. If your team of five or more depends on computers every day, managed is usually the more predictable option. See managed IT vs. an in-house hire and independent contractor vs. MSP for the trade-offs.

Business IT support FAQ

What is included in business IT support?

Help desk and remote support, device and Microsoft 365 administration, security basics such as multi-factor authentication and patching, backup monitoring, and a named point of contact, all under a written agreement.

How much does small business IT support cost?

$95 per user per month (minimum 5 users, 1-year agreement), delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately; on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. Offices with fewer than 5 users get custom billing on the same structure.

Which areas do you support?

Clearpine is based in Abbotsford and supports businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver, including Chilliwack, Mission, Maple Ridge, Langley, Surrey, Burnaby, New Westminster, Vancouver and North Vancouver. Day-to-day support is remote, with on-site visits as needed.

Is the IT assessment really free?

Yes. The assessment is free and no-obligation, on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford or remotely, for businesses of any size.

What is the difference between business IT support and a managed service provider?

Break-fix support responds when something breaks. A managed service provider (MSP) runs your IT proactively for a monthly fee, with monitoring, patching and security built in. Our managed IT support is the second model.
