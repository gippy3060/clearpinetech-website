Most small businesses do not need a full-time IT employee. They need someone reliable to answer when something breaks, and someone diligent enough that things break less often. Clearpine Technologies is a managed IT services provider (MSP) in Abbotsford serving businesses across the Lower Mainland, the Fraser Valley and the rest of Canada under a written service agreement.

What business IT support includes

  • Helpdesk. Reachable by phone and email, with defined response and resolution targets.
  • Endpoint management. Standardised builds and patching on a schedule rather than when someone remembers.
  • Microsoft 365 administration. User lifecycle, licensing, mailbox management and security hardening.
  • Backup verification. Backups tested, not merely scheduled. See why backups quietly fail.
  • Proactive monitoring. Disk, service and backup alerts so failures surface before users notice.
  • Asset and licence inventory. Kept current so renewals and end-of-life hardware do not ambush your budget.

Pricing

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users on a 1-year agreement, delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences and after-hours support are billed separately. On-site visits are billed separately, but free if we cannot fix the problem remotely. Business IT support: common questions

What does business IT support include?

Our managed IT support includes a helpdesk reachable by phone and email, endpoint management and patching, Microsoft 365 administration, backup verification, proactive monitoring and an up-to-date asset and licence inventory, delivered remotely during business hours under a written service agreement.

How much does small business IT support cost?

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users on a 1-year agreement. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, and on-site visits are free if we cannot fix the problem remotely.

Do you support small businesses in the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley?

Yes. We are based in Abbotsford and attend on site across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver, and we offer remote support across Canada.

Is there a free IT assessment?

Yes. We offer a free, no-obligation IT assessment on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford, for businesses of any size, with a short written summary of risks ranked by importance. Remote assessments are also available.

What is the difference between an MSP and an IT contractor?

A managed service provider (MSP) supports your business under a service agreement with a team, monitoring and documented processes, while a sole contractor depends on one person's availability. Our comparison is in the article on independent contractors versus MSPs.
