Business IT Support for Small Business in the Lower Mainland

Managed IT · Remote-first from Abbotsford · $95/user/month

Clearpine provides managed IT support for small and mid-sized businesses across the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley. You get a helpdesk your staff can actually reach, proactive monitoring and security, Microsoft 365 administration and tested backups, under one written agreement with a price you can read before you sign.

Support is remote-first from our Abbotsford office, which keeps costs down, with on-site visits scheduled when hands are needed.

What small business IT support includes

  • Helpdesk — staff call or email when something breaks and get a person who knows your setup.
  • Proactive monitoring and patching — laptops, servers and network gear kept updated before problems reach your team.
  • Cybersecurity — multi-factor authentication, firewall configuration, email protection and security basics scoped to a business your size.
  • Microsoft 365 administration — users, mailboxes, Teams, SharePoint and permissions managed for you.
  • Backup and recovery — backups that are tested, not just assumed to work.
  • Hardware and projects — new offices, relocations, workstation refreshes and network upgrades.
  • Co-managed IT — if you already have an IT person, we back them up with extra hands and coverage.

See the full list on our services page.

Business IT support pricing

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Support is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, but on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely.

Example: 10 users is 10 × $95 = $950 per month, before Microsoft 365 licences. For how that compares with a salaried hire, read managed IT vs in-house IT.

Who we support

Offices, professional firms and clinics that have outgrown “the person who is good with computers” but don’t need a full IT department. We have specific experience in regulated practices:

  • Medical clinics and dental offices
  • Physiotherapy, chiropractic and counselling clinics and veterinary clinics
  • Law firms

Where we work

We are headquartered in Abbotsford and support clients across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver: Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Langley, Surrey, Maple Ridge, Mission, Burnaby, New Westminster, Vancouver and North Vancouver. We don’t have an office in every one of those cities; day-to-day support is remote, with planned on-site visits.

Choosing an IT provider

Whoever you pick, ask for the price in writing, the contract length, what is excluded, how quickly someone answers, how backups are tested, and who holds your admin passwords. Our comparison of an independent contractor vs an MSP may also help.

Business IT support FAQ

What is business IT support?

Business IT support is ongoing help for the computers, network, email, security and cloud tools your staff rely on: a helpdesk to call, proactive monitoring and patching, backups that are tested, and someone accountable for keeping it all working.

How much does small business IT support cost in the Lower Mainland?

Clearpine managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement, delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately; on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely.

Do you support businesses across the Lower Mainland?

Yes. Clearpine is headquartered in Abbotsford and supports businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver remotely, with scheduled on-site visits as needed.

Is there a free way to find out what my business needs?

Yes. The free, no-obligation IT assessment can be done on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely, for a business of any size.

How do I choose an IT provider or MSP?

Ask for a written price, the contract length, what is and isn’t included, who answers the phone, how backups are tested, and who owns your admin credentials. A good provider answers all of these plainly before you sign.
