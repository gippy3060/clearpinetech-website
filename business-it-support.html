Most small businesses do not need an IT department. They need someone accountable for keeping staff working, devices patched, email protected and data recoverable. That is what a managed IT provider does, for a predictable monthly fee instead of surprise invoices.

Clearpine Technologies is an Abbotsford managed IT provider. We started in healthcare and public sector work, where privacy rules are strict and downtime is immediately visible, and we apply the same discipline to offices of every kind.

What is included

  • Help desk. Staff reach us by phone or email for everyday problems: logins, printers, slow computers, new starters and leavers.
  • Device management. Patching, endpoint protection and monitoring across workstations and laptops, so problems are found before staff notice.
  • Microsoft 365 administration. Users, licences, multi-factor authentication, email security and sensible sharing permissions. See Microsoft 365 support.
  • Backup you have actually tested. Recovery objectives agreed in advance and restores tested, not assumed. See why backups quietly fail.
  • Cybersecurity basics done properly. MFA, phishing protection, firewall and a plan for the day something goes wrong. See cybersecurity.
  • Network and Wi-Fi. Firewalls, switches and wireless that stay up, documented so the next person can understand them.
  • Planning. A written view of what to replace, upgrade or retire, ranked by risk and cost.

Business IT support: common questions

What is business IT support?

Business IT support is ongoing help with the technology a company relies on: a help desk for staff, device and Microsoft 365 management, security, backup and monitoring, plus someone accountable for planning upgrades. A managed service provider (MSP) delivers this for a monthly fee rather than charging for each call.

How much does small business IT support cost in the Lower Mainland?

Our managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. It is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, although on-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely. For businesses with fewer than 5 users, we can set up custom billing.

What is the difference between break-fix and managed IT?

Break-fix means you call and pay when something breaks. Managed IT is a monthly agreement where the provider monitors, patches and backs up your systems so fewer things break, and support is included. Break-fix can suit a very small office with simple needs; managed IT suits businesses where downtime costs real money.

How do I choose the best managed service provider in the Lower Mainland?

Ask for written pricing and what is excluded, defined response times, how backups are tested, who your point of contact is, how you leave if it does not work out, and for references you can call. Beware of any provider that cannot say in writing what is and is not covered. A free IT assessment is a low-risk way to compare how providers work.
