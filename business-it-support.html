Business IT Support for Small Businesses in the Lower Mainland & Fraser Valley

Help desk, security, Microsoft 365 and backups · from $95/user/month

Most small businesses don’t need an IT department. They need someone who answers when a laptop won’t log in, keeps email and backups working, and tells them plainly when something is a real risk and when it isn’t. That is what business IT support is, and it is what we do from our Abbotsford office for offices, clinics and professional firms across the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley.

What business IT support includes

  • Help desk — a real person to call or email when something stops working, so staff aren’t left fixing it themselves.
  • Devices and monitoring — laptops, desktops and servers kept patched and watched for problems before they turn into outages.
  • Microsoft 365 administration — user setup and removal, email, shared files, and access controls.
  • Security basics done properly — multi-factor authentication, firewall configuration, and protection against phishing and ransomware.
  • Backups that are tested — a backup nobody has restored from is only a hope. See why your backups are probably not working.
  • Network and Wi-Fi — office networks, printers and hardware setup for new offices and moves.

Full detail on each service is on our services page.

Business IT support pricing

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Support is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, but on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. For example, a 10-person office is 10 × $95 = $950 per month before Microsoft 365 licences. We would rather you see the number up front than wait for a quote to compare.

Who it’s for

Businesses that rely on computers every day but have no full-time IT person, or have one person who needs backup. If you already have an in-house IT lead, our co-managed IT covers after-hours, projects and holiday cover. Not sure which fits? Read managed IT vs in-house IT for small business.

Business IT support FAQ

What is business IT support?

Business IT support is the ongoing help and maintenance that keeps a company’s computers, network, email, security and backups working: a help desk for staff, monitoring and patching, Microsoft 365 administration, and a plan for when something breaks.

How much does small business IT support cost in BC?

Clearpine’s managed IT support is $95 per user per month (minimum 5 users, 1-year agreement), delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately; on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely.

Which areas do you support?

We are based in Abbotsford and support businesses across the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley, remotely day to day, with on-site visits as needed.

Can I get an IT assessment before committing?

Yes. The free, no-obligation IT assessment can be done on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford, for any business size, or remotely.
