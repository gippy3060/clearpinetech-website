Small Business IT Support in the Lower Mainland & Fraser Valley

Managed IT for offices of 5 users and up · Abbotsford, BC

Most small offices don’t need an IT department. They need someone who answers when the printer, the email or the VPN stops working, keeps the backups honest, and tells them plainly what is worth spending money on. That is what Clearpine does for businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver.

We are a managed service provider (MSP) headquartered in Abbotsford. Support is delivered remotely under a written service agreement, and we travel on site when something needs hands.

What business IT support includes

  • Helpdesk and remote support — a real person to call or email when staff are stuck.
  • Endpoint management — laptops and desktops patched, monitored and replaced on a sensible schedule.
  • Microsoft 365 administration — user setup and offboarding, mailboxes, shared drives, licences and security settings.
  • Cybersecurity — multi-factor authentication, firewall configuration, network segmentation and tested backups, scoped to what a small office actually needs.
  • Hardware and networking — workstations, servers, Wi-Fi and cabling for new offices, moves and refreshes.
  • Co-managed IT — backup for an existing in-house IT person: after-hours coverage, projects and holiday continuity.

Full detail is on our services page.

Pricing

Business IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Support is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, but on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. For offices with fewer than 5 users we offer custom billing on the same structure.

Free IT assessment

Not sure what you need? Our free IT assessment is no-obligation and can be done on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely, for a business of any size. We review backups, security, Microsoft 365, networking and the everyday problems your staff put up with, then tell you plainly what matters and what doesn’t.

Business IT support FAQ

What does business IT support include?
