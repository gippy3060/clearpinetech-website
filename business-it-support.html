Business IT Support for Small and Mid-Sized Companies in the Lower Mainland

Managed IT · Helpdesk · Security · Fraser Valley & Metro Vancouver

Most small businesses reach the same point: the person who “is good with computers” is overloaded, backups haven’t been tested, and nobody is sure who to call when email stops working. Clearpine provides business IT support for companies in the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley so that staff have a helpdesk, systems are maintained, and security is handled before it becomes an incident.

We are based in Abbotsford and work with offices from the Fraser Valley through Metro Vancouver. If you are comparing a managed service provider with an in-house hire or a break-fix contractor, see managed IT vs in-house for small business and independent contractor vs MSP.

What small business IT support includes

  • Helpdesk — staff get a person to call or email for everyday problems.
  • Device and user management — setup, patching and offboarding for laptops, desktops and accounts.
  • Microsoft 365 administration — mailboxes, shared files, licences and multi-factor authentication.
  • Backup and recovery — backups that are monitored and restore-tested, not assumed to work.
  • Network and security — firewall, Wi-Fi and segmentation configured to what your office actually needs.
  • Planning and projects — office moves, new sites and hardware refreshes. See our full list of services.

Managed IT, co-managed IT or project help

Not every business needs the same arrangement. A 10-person office usually wants a fully managed service. A company with an IT employee may only need co-managed IT for coverage and projects. Others need a one-time deployment or office build-out. The free assessment below is the quickest way to find out which fits.

Business IT support pricing

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Support is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, but on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. For offices with fewer than 5 users we offer custom billing on the same structure.

Free IT assessment

Our free IT assessment is no-obligation and can be done on site at any location within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely. We review backups, security, Microsoft 365, networking and the day-to-day problems your staff put up with, then tell you plainly what matters and what doesn’t.

Business IT support FAQ

What is business IT support?

Business IT support is ongoing help for the computers, network, email, security and backups a company depends on: a helpdesk for staff, proactive maintenance and monitoring, security configuration, and someone accountable when something breaks.

How much does small business IT support cost in the Lower Mainland?

Clearpine managed IT support is $95 per user per month, minimum 5 users, 1-year agreement. Support is remote during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, and on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. Offices with fewer than 5 users get custom billing on the same structure.

Is there a free way to find out what my business needs?

Yes. Our IT assessment is free and no-obligation. It can be done on site within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely, and covers backups, security, Microsoft 365, networking and day-to-day problems.

Do you support businesses outside Abbotsford?

Yes. We support businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver, and remotely beyond that.

Can you work alongside our existing IT person?

Yes. Co-managed IT covers after-hours support, project help and vacation or sick-day continuity for an in-house IT person or small team.

Tell us what’s not working and we’ll give you a straight read on what it takes to fix it — and what it costs.
