Business IT Support for Lower Mainland & Fraser Valley Offices

Small business IT support · Managed IT from $95/user/month

Most small and mid-sized offices don’t need a full-time IT department. They need someone dependable to answer when something breaks, and someone diligent enough that things break less often. That is what Clearpine provides: business IT support for offices across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver, run from Abbotsford under a written service agreement.

What business IT support includes

  • Helpdesk — your staff reach us by phone and email, with defined response and resolution targets reported against monthly.
  • Device management — standardised builds and patching on a schedule rather than when someone remembers.
  • Microsoft 365 administration — new starters and leavers, licensing, mailboxes and security hardening.
  • Backup verification — backups tested, not merely scheduled.
  • Proactive monitoring — disk, memory, service and backup alerts so failures surface before users notice.
  • Asset and licence inventory — so renewals and end-of-life hardware never ambush a budget.

Full detail is on our managed IT support and cybersecurity service pages.

Pricing

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Support is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, but on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. For a worked example, see our cost guides for dental offices and law firms; the arithmetic is the same for any office.

Who it’s for

  • Offices of about 5 to 30 people with no dedicated IT staff.
  • Regulated practices such as medical clinics, dental offices, allied health, veterinary clinics and law firms.
  • Teams with an IT person already who want backup: see co-managed IT under services.

Managed IT or hiring in-house?

If you are weighing the two, read managed IT vs. in-house IT for small businesses and independent contractor vs. MSP first. They set out the trade-offs without a sales pitch.

Where we work

We are headquartered in Abbotsford and support clients mostly remotely, travelling out when something needs hands on site. Local pages with travel times: Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Langley, Surrey, Burnaby, Vancouver, New Westminster, Maple Ridge, Mission and North Vancouver.
