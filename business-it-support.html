Small business IT support

Most small businesses do not need a full-time IT team. They need someone dependable to answer when something breaks, and someone diligent enough that it breaks less often. Clearpine Technologies is a managed service provider (MSP) based in Abbotsford, supporting offices across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland, from Abbotsford and Langley to Surrey and Vancouver.

What business IT support includes

  • Helpdesk. Reach us by phone and email, with defined response and resolution targets under a written service agreement.
  • Device management and patching. Standardised workstation builds and updates on a schedule rather than when someone remembers.
  • Microsoft 365 administration. New and departing staff, licensing, mailboxes and security hardening.
  • Backup verification. Backups are tested, not just scheduled. See why your backups probably are not working.
  • Monitoring. Disk, service and backup alerts so problems surface before your staff notice.
  • Security basics done properly. Multi-factor authentication, endpoint protection, firewall and email filtering. More on our cybersecurity service.
  • Projects. Office moves, new builds, hardware refreshes and migrations, scoped in writing first. See hardware deployment and project management.

Managed IT, co-managed IT or project work

Choose fully managed IT if you have no IT person, or co-managed IT if you do and want after-hours cover, project capacity and security tooling behind them. Weighing an employee against an outsourced team? Read managed IT vs in-house IT for small business and independent contractor vs MSP.

What it costs

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement, delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately. On-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely. Businesses with fewer than 5 users are welcome: we can set up custom billing. See on-site IT support costs in the Fraser Valley for more on what drives IT cost.

Where to start

Our free IT assessment reviews your security, Microsoft 365, backups and network and gives you a written, ranked list of what to fix. It is free with no obligation, and can be done on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely.

Business IT support: common questions

What does business IT support include?

Our managed IT support covers a helpdesk reachable by phone and email, workstation and device management, patching, Microsoft 365 administration, backup verification, proactive monitoring and an up-to-date asset and licence inventory. It is delivered remotely during business hours under a written service agreement.

How much does small business IT support cost in the Lower Mainland?

Our managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, although on-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely. For businesses with fewer than 5 users, we can set up custom billing.

Do you provide on-site IT support, or only remote?

Both. Most issues are fixed remotely. We are based in Abbotsford and can come on site across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland. If a problem cannot be fixed remotely, the on-site visit is free.

What is the difference between an MSP and an IT contractor?

A managed service provider (MSP) works under a service agreement with a team behind it, monitoring and maintaining your systems on a schedule. An independent contractor is often one person, billed as needed. We compare the two in our guide to independent contractor vs MSP.

Can you work alongside our in-house IT person?

Yes. Our co-managed IT service backs up an existing IT person or team with after-hours coverage, project capacity, security tooling and holiday cover.
