If you run a small or mid-sized business in the Lower Mainland, you probably want one thing from IT support: when something breaks, a person who knows your setup fixes it quickly, and the same person is already making sure it doesn’t break again. How to choose an IT support provider

Whoever you’re comparing, ask each one the same questions:

  • What is the per-user price, and what is billed on top (licences, after-hours, on-site visits)?
  • How long is the contract, and what happens if you want to leave?
  • Who answers the phone, and who comes on site?
  • How are backups tested, and when did they last restore a real file?
  • Can they show you what they would change in your setup before you sign?
