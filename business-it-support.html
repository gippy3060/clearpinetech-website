Small business IT support · Remote-first from Abbotsford · Fraser Valley to Vancouver

Clearpine is a managed service provider (MSP) based in Abbotsford. We look after IT for small and mid-sized businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver: help desk, Microsoft 365, security, backups and the hardware underneath, for one predictable monthly fee.

What small business IT support includes

  • Help desk — staff get a person to call or email when something breaks, with defined response times under a written service agreement.
  • Device and Microsoft 365 management — user setup and offboarding, patching, endpoint management and licence administration.
  • Cybersecurity — firewall configuration, multi-factor authentication, network segmentation and tested backups, scoped to what a small business actually needs.
  • Proactive monitoring — problems found and fixed before they stop anyone working.
  • Hardware and projects — servers, workstations, networking and cabling for new offices, moves and refreshes.
  • Co-managed IT — after-hours cover and project help for businesses that already have an in-house IT person.

How to choose an MSP in the Lower Mainland

Every MSP says it is the best, so ask questions that can be checked:

  • Is the price written down? Per-user pricing, what is excluded and the contract term should be clear before you talk to a salesperson.
  • What are the response times, in writing? A service agreement should say how fast a request is acknowledged and worked.
  • Are backups tested? Ask when the last restore test was. See why backups are probably not working.
  • Do they understand your sector? Clinics and law firms carry privacy and regulatory duties; see our pages for medical clinics, dental offices and law firms.
  • Who answers, and where? Know whether support is local, how on-site visits work and what they cost.
  • Can you leave? Ask what you get back (documentation, admin credentials) if you end the agreement.

Business IT support FAQ

What is a managed service provider (MSP)?

An MSP runs your day-to-day IT for a fixed monthly fee instead of charging per incident or requiring a full-time hire.

How much does small business IT support cost?

$95 per user per month (minimum 5 users, 1-year agreement). Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately; on-site visits are free when a problem can’t be fixed remotely.

Do you support businesses outside Abbotsford?

Yes. We support businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver, remotely with on-site visits as needed.

Can you work alongside our existing IT person?

Yes. Co-managed IT covers after-hours support, projects and holiday or sick-day continuity.
