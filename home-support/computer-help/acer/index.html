Try it yourself first

Acer laptop won't turn on: what to check

Steps can differ a little between models. If these do not help, call us.

  1. Charge it first. Plug the charger into a wall outlet you know works, not a power bar with a switch. Leave it for about 15 minutes. Check that the small light near the charging port or on the front comes on.
  2. Unplug everything else. Take out USB sticks, memory cards, mice and any other cables. Sometimes one of these stops a laptop from starting.
  3. Hold the power button. Press and hold it for about 10 to 15 seconds, let go, wait a moment, then press it once. A laptop that looked "dead" was sometimes only asleep or stuck.
  4. Look for the battery reset hole. Many Acer laptops have a tiny hole on the bottom, often with a battery symbol next to it. Unplug the charger. Gently press inside the hole with a straightened paper clip for a few seconds. Then plug the charger back in and press the power button. This does not erase your files.
  5. Check the screen, not just the lights. If the lights come on and you hear the fan but the screen stays black, shine a flashlight on the screen or connect a TV or monitor. The computer may be running with a screen problem.
  6. If it starts, install updates. Open Settings, then Windows Update, and install what is waiting. Then restart once more.
  7. Know when to stop. If there are no lights at all with the charger plugged in, the charger or a part inside may have failed. If the case is bulging or the touchpad is lifting, the battery may be swollen. That is a safety risk: stop using the laptop and have it looked at.

Touchpad not working? On many Acer laptops, holding Fn and pressing the top-row key with a touchpad picture (often F7) turns it off and on. Try that once, then check Settings, Bluetooth and devices, Touchpad.

More general steps are on our computer help page. Using a different laptop? See independent ASUS help, independent Dell help, independent HP computer help, independent Lenovo help or independent Microsoft Surface help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Acer computers

Acer Care Center and AcerSense. Many Acer computers come with an Acer app called Acer Care Center. Some newer models use one called AcerSense instead. These apps show battery health, check for Acer updates, run a simple check-up and show your serial number. They are normal and come from the maker.

Battery charge limit. Some Acer laptops can stop charging at about 80 percent to help the battery last longer. If your laptop is plugged in most of the time, that is a good thing. If you need a full charge before a trip, you can turn it off in the Acer app.

Gaming models have their own app. Nitro and Predator laptops use apps called NitroSense or PredatorSense for fans, lights and power modes. A quiet or power-saving mode there can make a gaming laptop feel slow. We can explain the modes in plain words.

Serial number and SNID. The sticker underneath usually shows the serial number (S/N) and a shorter number called the SNID. If a driver is needed, we use Windows Update, the Acer app or Acer's own website for your model. Please do not download "driver updater" programs from search results or pop-ups; they are a common trick.

Acer Chromebooks are different. This page is about Acer computers running Windows. If you have an Acer Chromebook, call and we will tell you honestly whether we can help before any session starts.

Nobody from Acer or Microsoft will call you. Calls and pop-ups about a virus are scams. If you let someone in, see virus and scam clean-up and read how to tell if a tech support call is a scam.

Questions

Acer computer help FAQ

My Acer laptop will not turn on. What can I do?

Plug in the charger and wait about 15 minutes. Unplug any USB sticks or other devices. Many Acer laptops have a tiny battery reset hole on the bottom, often marked with a battery symbol. With the charger unplugged, press inside it gently with a straightened paper clip for a few seconds, then plug the charger back in and press the power button. This does not erase your files. If nothing happens, call us and we will go through the next steps with you.

My Acer battery stops charging at 80 percent. Is it broken?

Probably not. Some Acer laptops have a battery charge limit setting that stops charging at about 80 percent to help the battery last longer. It is usually in the Acer Care Center app or, on some newer models, the AcerSense app. If you need a full charge before a trip, you can turn it off. We can show you where it is on your model.

The touchpad on my Acer laptop stopped working. What can I check?

On many Acer laptops, holding the Fn key and pressing a top-row key with a touchpad picture (often F7) turns the touchpad off and on. Press it once and try again. Also check Windows Settings, Bluetooth and devices, Touchpad, and make sure the touchpad is switched on. Then restart.

What is Acer Care Center or AcerSense? Is it safe?

These are Acer's own apps that come with many Acer computers. They show battery health, check for Acer updates, run a simple check-up and show your serial number. They are normal. A pop-up from a website that says it is Acer and tells you to call a number is not; that is a scam.

Where do I find my Acer serial number?

It is usually on a sticker underneath the laptop, marked S/N. The same sticker often has a shorter number called the SNID. Acer Care Center or AcerSense also shows them. You may need one to check your warranty on Acer's own website.

I have an Acer Chromebook. Is this page for me?

This page is about Acer computers running Windows. Chromebooks work differently and have fewer settings to go wrong. Call us and we will tell you honestly whether we can help with your Chromebook problem before any session starts.

Someone called saying they are from Microsoft or Acer and my computer has a virus. Is that real?

No. This is a common scam. Neither Microsoft nor Acer calls people about viruses, and neither do we. Hang up. Do not let the caller connect to your computer or pay them anything. If you already let them in, see our virus and scam clean-up page, and report it to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre in Canada or to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov in the USA.
