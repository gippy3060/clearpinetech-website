Try it yourself first

ASUS touchpad not working: what to check

Steps can differ a little between models. If these do not help, call us.

  1. Look for the touchpad key. Many ASUS laptops have a key on the top row with a small touchpad picture. Press it once. If nothing changes, hold the Fn key and press it. This key is easy to press by accident.
  2. Unplug any mouse. On some settings, the touchpad turns off when a mouse is plugged in. Unplug it and try the touchpad again.
  3. Check the Windows setting. If you have a mouse, open Settings, then Bluetooth and devices, then Touchpad, and make sure Touchpad is switched on.
  4. Restart the laptop. Click Start, then Power, then Restart. A restart often brings the touchpad back.
  5. Check for updates. Open Settings, then Windows Update, and install what is waiting. The MyASUS app can also check for ASUS updates. Then restart again.
  6. If the laptop froze completely, press and hold the power button until it turns off, wait a moment, then press it once to turn it back on. Your files stay where they are.
  7. Know when to stop. If the touchpad still does not respond, or the case near it is bulging, the touchpad or battery may need a repair. A swollen battery is a safety risk: stop using the laptop and have it looked at.

More general steps are on our computer help page. Using a different laptop? See independent Dell help, independent HP computer help, independent Lenovo help, independent Microsoft Surface help or independent Acer help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to ASUS computers

The MyASUS app. Most ASUS laptops come with this app from ASUS. It holds battery charge settings, keyboard settings, system updates and a hardware check, and it shows your serial number. It is normal and comes from the maker.

Battery Health Charging. Many ASUS laptops can stop charging at about 60 or 80 percent to help the battery last longer. If your laptop is plugged in most of the time, that is a good thing. If you need a full charge before a trip, you can change it in MyASUS.

Gaming models have their own app. ROG and some TUF laptops use an app called Armoury Crate for fans, lights and power modes. A "silent" or power-saving mode there can make a gaming laptop feel slow. We can explain the modes in plain words.

Serial number and drivers. The serial number (S/N) is on a sticker underneath. If a driver is needed, we use Windows Update, MyASUS or ASUS's own website for your model. Please do not download "driver updater" programs from search results or pop-ups; they are a common trick.

ASUS also makes routers. This page is about ASUS computers. For Wi-Fi trouble, see our Wi-Fi and router help.

Nobody from ASUS or Microsoft will call you. Calls and pop-ups about a virus are scams. If you let someone in, see virus and scam clean-up and read how to tell if a tech support call is a scam.

Questions

ASUS computer help FAQ

My ASUS laptop battery stops charging at 60 or 80 percent. Is it broken?

Probably not. Many ASUS laptops have a battery-saving setting, often called Battery Health Charging or battery care, that stops charging partway to help the battery last longer. It is usually in the MyASUS app. If you need a full charge, for example before a trip, you can change it. We can show you where it is on your model.

The touchpad on my ASUS laptop stopped working. What can I check?

On many ASUS laptops a function key with a touchpad picture turns the touchpad off and on. Press it once, or hold the Fn key and press it. Also check Windows Settings, Bluetooth and devices, Touchpad, and make sure the touchpad is switched on. If a mouse is plugged in, try unplugging it. Then restart.

My ASUS laptop will not turn on. What can I do?

Plug in the charger and wait about 15 minutes. Unplug any USB sticks or other devices. Then press and hold the power button for about 40 seconds on many models, let go, wait a moment and press it once to turn it on. This kind of reset does not erase your files. If nothing happens, call us and we will go through the next steps with you.

My F keys or brightness keys do the wrong thing. Why?

Many ASUS laptops have a function lock. When it is on, the top-row keys do one job, and when it is off, they do the other. On many models you switch it by pressing Fn and Esc together. The MyASUS app may also have this setting.

Where do I find my ASUS serial number?

It is usually on a sticker underneath the laptop, marked S/N. The MyASUS app also shows it. You may need it to check your warranty on ASUS's own website.

Someone called saying they are from Microsoft or ASUS and my computer has a virus. Is that real?

No. This is a common scam. Neither Microsoft nor ASUS calls people about viruses, and neither do we. Hang up. Do not let the caller connect to your computer or pay them anything. If you already let them in, see our virus and scam clean-up page, and report it to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre in Canada or to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov in the USA.
