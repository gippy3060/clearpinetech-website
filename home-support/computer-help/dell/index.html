Try it yourself first

Dell laptop won't charge: what to check

Menu names can differ a little between models and Windows versions. If these do not help, call us.

  1. Check the wall socket. Plug a lamp or phone charger into the same socket to be sure it has power. Try a different socket or a different power strip.
  2. Look at the charger. Check the cable for kinks, cuts or a bent tip. A charger that feels very hot or smells burnt should not be used.
  3. Re-seat the plug. Unplug the charger from the wall and from the laptop, wait a minute, then plug the wall end in first and the laptop end second.
  4. Check the charging port. Look for lint or dirt in the port. Do not push anything metal inside. If the plug wiggles loosely, the port may be worn.
  5. Use the right charger. The wattage and plug type are printed on the charger and differ between Dell models. A laptop may show a message about the power adapter, or charge slowly, if the charger is the wrong one.
  6. Restart. Shut the laptop down fully and start it again with the charger connected.
  7. Know when to stop. If it still will not charge, the charger, port or battery probably needs replacing. We will tell you honestly; that is a repair-shop job.

More general steps are on our computer help page. For email or account trouble, see email and account help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Dell computers

Service Tag. Every Dell has a short Service Tag on a sticker, usually on the bottom of a laptop or the back or side of a desktop. Dell's own website uses it to show your exact model, its manual and the right drivers. Keep a photo of it on your phone.

Drivers from Dell only. If a driver is needed, we use Dell's own website for your model. Please do not download "driver updater" programs from search results or pop-ups; they are a common trick.

Preinstalled Dell apps. Many Dells come with Dell's own support and update apps. They are normal and come from the maker. Whether to keep using them is your choice, and we can show you what each one does.

BIOS updates. The BIOS is the small program that starts the computer before Windows. We only change it when there is a clear reason, with the charger plugged in.

Older Dells and Windows 11. Some older Dell computers do not meet the requirements for Windows 11. That is a limit of the hardware, not a fault. We can explain your options in plain words.

Questions

Dell help FAQ

My Dell laptop is slow. What can I try first?

Restart it fully (Start, Power, Restart). Then open Settings, System, Storage and check free space; a nearly full drive slows Windows down. Open Task Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc), click Startup apps, and switch off programs you do not use. If it is still slow, call us and we will look at it with you.

Where do I find my Dell Service Tag?

It is a short code of letters and numbers on a sticker, usually on the bottom of a laptop or the back or side of a desktop. In Windows you can also see it in Settings, System, About, or by opening the preinstalled Dell app if you have one. Dell uses it on its own website to show your exact model and drivers.

My Dell says "plugged in, not charging" or it will not recognise the charger. What now?

Unplug the charger from the wall and the laptop, wait a minute, and plug it back in, wall end first. Check the cable for kinks and the tip for damage. Try a different wall socket. A laptop can show a message about the power adapter if the charger is not the right one for that model. If it still does not charge, the charger, charging port or battery may need replacing, which remote help cannot do.

My Dell will not start or shows a black screen. Can you help?

If you see nothing at all, hold the power button for about 15 seconds with the charger unplugged, plug in the charger and try again. If Windows starts but the screen is dark, we may be able to help. If the laptop does not power on or shows a hardware error before Windows loads, it usually needs a repair shop. We will tell you honestly.

Should I update the BIOS on my Dell?

Only if there is a clear reason, such as a fix for a problem you have. A BIOS update interrupted by a power cut or a flat battery can stop a computer from starting. If it is needed we will go slowly with you, with the charger plugged in, and only download it from Dell's own website.
