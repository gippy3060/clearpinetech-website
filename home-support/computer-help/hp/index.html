Try it yourself first

HP laptop touchpad not working: what to check

Not every HP has every feature below. Go through them in order. If these do not help, call us.

  1. Plug in a mouse if you have one. It makes the next steps easier. A plain USB mouse works without any setup.
  2. Look at the top-left corner of the touchpad. On many HP laptops a small light there means the touchpad is switched off. Double-tap that corner quickly to switch it back on.
  3. Look along the top row of keys. Some HP models have a key with a touchpad picture on it. Press it once, or hold Fn and press it.
  4. Check the Windows setting. Open Start, then Settings, Bluetooth and devices, Touchpad. Make sure the Touchpad switch is on. If there is a box for "Leave touchpad on when a mouse is connected", tick it.
  5. Restart. Use Start, Power, Restart, not just closing the lid.
  6. Think back. Did it stop right after a Windows update? A driver may need to be put back. We do that from HP's own website for your exact model, never from a "driver updater" pop-up.
  7. Know when to stop. If the pointer jumps around by itself, or the touchpad has lifted or looks raised, stop and turn the laptop off. A swollen battery can push the touchpad up, and that needs a repair shop, not remote help.

More general steps are on our computer help page. Using a different laptop? See independent Dell help or independent Lenovo help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to HP computers

HP Support Assistant. Most HP computers come with this app from HP. It shows your serial and product numbers and checks for HP updates. It is a normal part of the computer.

Serial and product number. Look for S/N and P/N on the sticker underneath a laptop or on the back of a desktop. If a driver is needed, we use HP's own website for your exact model. Please do not download "driver updater" programs from search results or pop-ups; they are a common trick.

Built-in hardware test. Many HPs include HP PC Hardware Diagnostics. On many models you open it by pressing Esc again and again as the computer starts, then choosing diagnostics. It is safe to run and only tests the parts.

Start-up menu. That same Esc menu also has recovery and reset options. Please do not choose those on your own; some of them can erase the computer. We will walk through it together if it is ever needed.

Pre-installed programs. New HPs often come with trial security software and other extras. We help you decide what to keep, so you do not end up with two antivirus programs fighting each other.

Questions

HP computer help FAQ

The touchpad on my HP laptop stopped working. What can I check?

On many HP laptops, double-tapping the top-left corner of the touchpad switches it off or on, and a small light in that corner shows it is off. Some models have a touchpad key on the top row instead. If neither helps, open Windows Settings, Bluetooth and devices, Touchpad, and make sure it is switched on. A plugged-in mouse can also be used in the meantime.

What is the HP Support Assistant app on my laptop?

It is an app from HP that comes on most HP computers. It shows your serial and product numbers, checks for HP updates and drivers, and can run some basic tests. It is a normal part of the computer, not a virus.

Where do I find my HP serial number and product number?

On a laptop it is usually on a sticker underneath or inside the battery area; on a desktop it is on the back or side. Look for S/N for the serial number and P/N or Product for the product number. The HP Support Assistant app also shows both. HP's own website uses them to show your exact model, manuals and drivers.

How can I tell if my HP laptop has a hardware problem?

Many HP computers have a built-in test called HP PC Hardware Diagnostics. On many models you reach it by pressing Esc repeatedly as the computer starts, then choosing the diagnostics option. It checks parts such as the memory and drive. We can walk you through it and read the results with you.

My HP laptop is slow. What can I try first?

Restart it fully (Start, Power, Restart). Then open Settings, System, Storage and check free space; a nearly full drive slows Windows down. Open Task Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc), click Startup apps, and switch off programs you do not use. If it is still slow, call us and we will look at it with you.
