Try this first

A slow computer: five things worth checking

  1. Restart it properly. On Windows choose Start, then Power, then Restart (not Shut down, which can keep some settings from fully resetting). On a Mac use the Apple menu, then Restart.
  2. Check your free space. On Windows open Settings, System, Storage. On a Mac open the Apple menu, System Settings, General, Storage. If the drive is nearly full, the computer slows down.
  3. Look at what starts with the computer. On Windows press Ctrl + Shift + Esc and open the Startup tab. On a Mac see System Settings, General, Login Items. Turn off things you do not use.
  4. Install pending updates. Windows Update or macOS Software Update, then restart.
  5. Check the browser. Remove extensions you do not recognise, and close tabs you are not using.

If it is still slow, call us. A very old computer or a failing drive may need a hardware fix that cannot be done remotely, and we will tell you honestly.

What remote support can and cannot do

Being honest about limits

  • We can fix software, settings, accounts and most slowdowns remotely.
  • We cannot repair a cracked screen, a broken keyboard or a drive that has physically failed. For failed drives, a data recovery lab is needed.
  • We can try to recover deleted files and photos from the recycle bin, backups and cloud services. Recovery is not always possible.
  • If we cannot fix it remotely, you are not charged.
Questions

Computer help questions

Why is my computer so slow?

Common causes are too many programs starting with the computer, a nearly full drive, pending updates, an old browser full of extensions, or unwanted software. We look at what is actually running and fix what we find. An older computer with a failing drive or very little memory may need a hardware upgrade, and we will tell you if that is the case.

Can you move my files to a new computer?

Yes, in most cases. We can help copy your documents, photos, browser bookmarks and email to a new Windows PC or Mac while you watch. Files must be readable on the old computer; a drive that has physically failed needs a data recovery lab.

How much does it cost?

A one-time remote session is a flat $100 CAD, for customers in Canada and the United States. If we cannot fix the problem remotely, you are not charged.

Is it safe to let you connect to my computer?

A remote session only starts when you agree to it, and you can watch everything on your screen. You can end the session at any time. We never call, text or email you first.

Can you remove a virus?

We can check for malware and remove it where possible; see our virus and scam clean-up page. For email problems, see email and account help. If you were contacted by a scammer, see how to spot a tech-support scam. We never show fake virus warnings.
