Try it yourself first

Lenovo battery stops at 60 percent or won't charge: what to check

Menu names can differ a little between models and app versions. If these do not help, call us.

  1. Look at the number. If the battery always stops at about the same level, often near 60 percent, and says it is plugged in, a battery-saving setting is the likely reason. This is normal and helps the battery last longer.
  2. Open Lenovo Vantage. Most Lenovo laptops come with this app from Lenovo. Look in its power or battery section for a setting called Conservation Mode or a charge threshold. Switch it off if you want a full charge, and back on if the laptop stays plugged in most of the time.
  3. If it does not charge at all, check the socket. Plug a lamp or phone charger into the same socket. Try a different socket or power strip.
  4. Look at the charger. Check the cable for kinks, cuts or a damaged tip. Many newer Lenovos charge through a USB-C port; make sure you are using the charging port, not another one. A charger that feels very hot or smells burnt should not be used.
  5. Use the right charger. The wattage is printed on the charger. A small phone charger may not be strong enough for a laptop, even if the plug fits.
  6. Restart. Shut the laptop down fully and start it again with the charger connected.
  7. Know when to stop. If it still will not charge, the charger, port or battery probably needs replacing. We will tell you honestly; that is a repair-shop job.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Lenovo computers

Lenovo Vantage. Most Lenovos come with this app from Lenovo. It holds battery, keyboard, camera and update settings, and shows your serial number. Some ThinkPads use a Lenovo update tool instead. Both are normal and come from the maker.

Camera cover. Many Lenovo laptops have a small slide over the camera, above the screen. It is a privacy feature. If the camera picture is dark, check it first.

Function keys. The top row usually controls volume, brightness and other shortcuts first. On many models, Fn and Esc together switch it back to normal F1 to F12 keys.

Serial number and drivers. The serial number (S/N) and machine type (MT) are on a sticker underneath. If a driver is needed, we use Lenovo's own website for your model. Please do not download "driver updater" programs from search results or pop-ups; they are a common trick.

Small recovery button. Some IdeaPads have a tiny pinhole button on the side for start-up and recovery menus. Please leave it alone unless we are walking through it together; some options there can erase the computer.

Questions

Lenovo help FAQ

My Lenovo laptop battery stops charging at about 60 percent. Is it broken?

Probably not. Many Lenovo laptops have a battery-saving setting, often called Conservation Mode or a charge threshold, that stops charging partway to make the battery last longer. It is found in the Lenovo Vantage app on most models. If you need a full charge, for example before a trip, you can switch it off. We can show you where it is.

The camera on my Lenovo shows a black or grey picture. What can I check?

Many Lenovo laptops have a small sliding cover over the camera, above the screen. If it is closed, the camera shows a dark or crossed-out picture. Slide it open. Some models also have a camera on/off key on the keyboard. If it is open and still dark, check the camera privacy settings in Windows Settings, Privacy and security, Camera.

The F keys on my Lenovo change the volume instead of doing what I expect. How do I fix it?

Lenovo laptops often use the top row for volume, brightness and other shortcuts first. On many models, pressing Fn and Esc together switches the top row between shortcuts and normal F1 to F12 keys. A small light on the Esc key may show which mode is on. The setting can also be in Lenovo Vantage.

Where do I find my Lenovo serial number and model?

It is on a sticker, usually on the bottom of a laptop or the back of a desktop, often labelled S/N for serial number and MT or MTM for machine type. The Lenovo Vantage app also shows it. Lenovo's own website uses it to show your exact model, manuals and drivers.

My Lenovo laptop is slow. What can I try first?

Restart it fully (Start, Power, Restart). Then open Settings, System, Storage and check free space; a nearly full drive slows Windows down. Open Task Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc), click Startup apps, and switch off programs you do not use. If it is still slow, call us and we will look at it with you.
