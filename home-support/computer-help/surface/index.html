Common Surface problems

Plain steps, no rush. You can ask us to repeat anything. This covers Surface Pro, Surface Laptop, Surface Go and other Surface computers running Windows.

Keyboard stopped working

A Surface Pro keyboard clips on with magnets. Dust on the contacts or a loose fit can stop it typing. We go through the checks with you.

Try it yourself first

Surface Pro keyboard not working: what to check

Steps can differ a little between models. If these do not help, call us.

  1. Take the keyboard off and put it back. Pull it gently away from the bottom edge of the Surface, wait a few seconds, then let the magnets click it back into place. Make sure it sits straight and flat.
  2. Clean the contacts. Look at the small metal contacts on the keyboard edge and along the bottom of the Surface. Wipe both with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use liquid or anything sharp.
  3. Restart the Surface. Click Start, then Power, then Restart. A restart often brings the keyboard back.
  4. Use the on-screen keyboard meanwhile. If you need to type a password to get in, tap the keyboard symbol on the sign-in screen to bring up a keyboard on the screen.
  5. Check for updates. Microsoft sends Surface fixes through Windows Update. Open Settings, then Windows Update, and install what is waiting. Then restart again.
  6. If the Surface froze completely, press and hold the power button for about 20 seconds on most models, and let go when the logo appears. This forces a restart without erasing your files.
  7. Know when to stop. If the keyboard still does not work, the keyboard or the connector may be damaged. We will tell you honestly; a replacement keyboard or a repair may be needed.

More general steps are on our computer help page. Using a different laptop? See independent Dell help, independent HP computer help or independent Lenovo help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Surface computers

The magnetic charger. Many Surfaces charge through a thin magnetic connector on the side. It should sit flat and snug. On many models a small light on the connector shows that power is reaching it. Newer models can also charge through USB-C, but a small phone charger may not be strong enough.

The Surface app. This app from Microsoft shows your serial number, warranty and pen settings. On a Surface Pro, the serial number is usually printed under the kickstand.

Updates come through Windows Update. Surface drivers and firmware arrive the same way as Windows updates. You do not need a separate "driver updater" program. Please do not download those from search results or pop-ups; they are a common trick.

Some newer Surfaces use a different kind of chip. Some recent Surface models use an Arm processor instead of the usual Intel or AMD chip. Most apps run fine, but some older programs, games or printer and scanner drivers may not. Before you move to a new Surface, we can check that the programs and printer you rely on will work.

Microsoft will not call you. Calls and pop-ups claiming to be from Microsoft about a virus are scams. If you let someone in, see virus and scam clean-up and read how to tell if a tech support call is a scam.

Questions

Microsoft Surface help FAQ

The keyboard on my Surface Pro stopped working. What can I check?

Surface Pro keyboards clip on with magnets. Take the keyboard off, gently wipe the metal contacts on the keyboard and along the bottom edge of the Surface with a dry, soft cloth, then click it back on. Restart the Surface. If the keyboard works on another Surface, or another keyboard works on yours, that tells us which part has the problem.

My Surface will not charge. What should I try?

Plug the charger into a different wall socket, not a power strip. Unplug the magnetic charging connector, check for dust or bent pins, and plug it back in so it sits flat. On many models a small light on the connector turns on when power is reaching it. Newer Surfaces can also charge through USB-C with a strong enough charger. If nothing works, the charger, port or battery may need replacing.

My Surface battery stops at 80 percent. Is something wrong?

Probably not. Many Surfaces have a battery-saving feature, often called Smart Charging or a battery limit, that stops at about 80 percent when the device is plugged in for long periods. It helps the battery last longer. The setting is usually in the Surface app or the device's start-up settings. We can show you where it is on your model.

My Surface screen is black and it will not turn on. What can I do?

Plug in the charger and wait about 15 minutes. Then, on most models, press and hold the power button for about 20 seconds and let go when the Windows or Surface logo appears. This forces a restart without erasing anything. If the screen stays black, call us and we will go through the next steps with you.

Where do I find my Surface serial number?

On a Surface Pro it is usually printed on the back, under the kickstand. On a Surface Laptop it is usually on the bottom. The Surface app from Microsoft also shows it. You may need it to check your warranty on Microsoft's own website.

Someone called saying they are from Microsoft and my computer has a virus. Is that real?

No. This is one of the most common scams. Microsoft does not call people about viruses, and neither do we. Hang up. Do not let the caller connect to your computer or pay them anything. If you already let them in, see our virus and scam clean-up page, and report it to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre in Canada or to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov in the USA.
