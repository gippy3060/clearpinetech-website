What we help with

Three kinds of home tech problems

Computer and software help

Windows and Mac problems, a slow computer, installing or updating software, email setup (Outlook, Gmail), Microsoft 365, antivirus, virus and malware clean-up, moving files to a new PC, and setting up backups.

Wi-Fi, router and extender help

Setting up or troubleshooting routers and mesh systems from D-Link, Linksys, Netgear, TP-Link, ASUS, Eero, Google Nest Wifi and internet-provider routers (Rogers, Bell, Telus, Shaw, Videotron). Weak Wi-Fi, extenders, password changes, firmware updates and parental controls. Read more about Wi-Fi and router help.

Independent printer help

Wi-Fi printer setup, "printer offline" errors, driver installs, scanning, and connecting printers to phones and laptops. Independent printer support for HP, Brother, Canon, Epson, Lexmark, Xerox, Samsung and more. Read more about printer help.

How it works

Simple, and you stay in control

1

Call or email

Tell us what is going wrong. We will say whether remote support can help.

2

You allow the connection

A remote session starts only when you say yes. You can watch everything on your screen.

3

We fix it with you

We work through the problem and explain what we are doing in plain language.

4

Pay one flat fee

$100 CAD for the session, and nothing if we cannot fix the problem. You can end the session at any time.

Price

$100 CAD flat, one-time session

One price, no subscription. Available anywhere in Canada, from Vancouver to Halifax and from Toronto and Montreal to smaller towns and rural areas, as long as you have a working internet connection.
