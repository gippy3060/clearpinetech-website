Try it yourself first

Brother printer offline: what to check

Menus and buttons differ by model, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Look at the printer's screen. Many Brother printers show a Wi-Fi symbol or a signal-strength indicator on the screen. If it is missing or crossed out, the printer is not on your network.
  2. Restart in order. Turn off the printer, router and computer. Turn on the router, wait two minutes, then the printer, then the computer.
  3. Run the wireless setup again. On many models with a screen, look in the Menu for Network, then WLAN or Wi-Fi, then the setup wizard. It lists nearby networks. Choose yours and type the Wi-Fi password carefully, as it is case-sensitive.
  4. Use the same Wi-Fi. Your computer or phone and the printer must be on the same network. Guest networks, and some extender setups, can block printers.
  5. Try a Wi-Fi report. Many Brother printers can print a wireless report from the Network or Print Reports menu. It includes an error code that helps show whether the password, the signal or the router is the problem.
  6. Download only from Brother's own website. Brother offers free drivers, software and a network repair tool for some models on its support website. Never download printer software from a pop-up, an advert or a link in a message.
  7. Remove and add the printer again. In Windows: Settings, Bluetooth & devices, Printers & scanners. On a Mac: System Settings, Printers & Scanners.

More general steps are on our printer help page. If your Wi-Fi is weak where the printer sits, see Wi-Fi extender and mesh setup help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Brother printers

Toner and drum are separate. Most Brother laser printers use a toner cartridge and a separate drum unit. A drum lasts through several toner cartridges. A message about either one does not always mean you need to buy something, and some models need a reset step after a new part goes in.

Two kinds of Brother printers. Laser printers (often black and white) and inkjet printers behave a little differently. The model number on the front or back of the printer tells us which you have.

Brother apps. Brother has free apps for phones and tablets for printing and scanning. Which app suits you depends on the printer model, and some Brother printers also work with the built-in printing on iPhone, iPad, Android and Mac.

Remote tools. We connect to a Windows computer with Quick Assist, or to a Mac with LogMeIn. You start it, you see everything we do, and you can end it at any time. The printer's own screen and buttons are guided by voice.

What remote support cannot do

  • Repair hardware faults such as paper jams from worn rollers or a worn drum
  • Supply ink, toner, drums or parts
  • Make a very old printer work with a new operating system if Brother no longer provides a driver

We will tell you honestly if the problem looks like hardware. If we can't fix the problem remotely, there is no charge.

Questions

Brother printer help FAQ

My Brother printer says offline. What should I do?

Turn off the printer, router and computer, then turn on the router first, the printer second and the computer last. Then check on the printer's screen that it shows a Wi-Fi connection. If it does not, the printer has lost your network and needs to be reconnected. If it still says offline, call us and we will check the connection with you.

What is Brother iPrint&Scan and do I need it?

Brother iPrint&Scan is a free app from Brother for phones and tablets that lets you print and scan over Wi-Fi. Brother also has a newer app called Brother Mobile Connect. Which one suits your printer depends on the model. If you are not sure, we will help you find out and walk you through it.

My Brother printer says to replace the toner or drum, but I just did. Why?

Brother laser printers have two parts that wear out: the toner cartridge and the drum unit. The message can refer to either one, and some models need a short reset step after a new part is fitted. We can talk you through checking which part the message means. We cannot repair a worn drum or supply parts.

Can you fix scanning on my Brother printer?

Often yes. Scanning to a computer usually depends on the printer and the computer being on the same network and on Brother's scanning software or the built-in scanner feature of Windows or Mac. We can check each step with you.
