Try it yourself first

Brother printer offline: what to check

Menus and buttons differ by model, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Look at the printer's screen. Many Brother printers show a Wi-Fi signal symbol on the screen. No symbol, or an empty one, usually means the printer is not connected to your Wi-Fi.
  2. Restart in order. Turn off the printer, router and computer. Turn on the router, wait two minutes, then the printer, then the computer.
  3. Print a wireless report. On many models you can print a WLAN or wireless report from the Menu or Settings, under Print Reports. It shows whether the printer found your network, and if not, an error code.
  4. Use the same Wi-Fi. Your computer or phone and the printer must be on the same network. Guest networks, and some extender setups, can block printers.
  5. Run the Wi-Fi setup wizard again. If you changed your Wi-Fi password, the printer needs to be told the new one. On many models this is under Network, then WLAN, then Setup Wizard. Have your Wi-Fi name and password ready.
  6. Check which printer your computer is using. After a re-install, a computer can end up with two copies of the same printer, one that works and one marked offline. Remove the extra one. In Windows: Settings, Bluetooth & devices, Printers & scanners. On a Mac: System Settings, Printers & Scanners.
  7. Get software only from Brother's own website. If you need a driver or scanning software, type the address yourself. Never download it from a pop-up, an advert or a link in a message.

More general steps are on our printer help page. If your Wi-Fi is weak where the printer sits, see Wi-Fi extender and mesh setup help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Brother printers

Toner and drum are separate. Most Brother laser printers use a toner cartridge and a separate drum unit. A "Replace Toner" or "Replace Drum" message may be about either one, and the printer may keep a count of pages for each. We can help you work out which message you have. We cannot supply parts.

The printer's own password. Many newer Brother printers have a unique password for the printer's settings page, printed on a label on the back or bottom of the printer. It is different from your Wi-Fi password.

Printers, scanners and fax. Many Brother models are all-in-one machines. Printing, scanning and fax can each have their own settings, so one can work while another does not.

What remote support cannot do

  • Repair hardware faults such as worn paper rollers, a failed drum or a broken scanner glass
  • Supply toner, ink, drums or parts
  • Make a very old printer work with a new operating system if Brother no longer provides a driver

We will tell you honestly if the problem looks like hardware. If we can't fix the problem remotely, there is no charge.

Questions

Brother printer help FAQ

My Brother printer says offline. What should I do?

Turn off the printer, router and computer, then turn on the router first, the printer second and the computer last. Then check the printer's screen for a Wi-Fi signal indicator. If it shows no signal, the printer is not connected to your Wi-Fi. If it still says offline, call us and we will check the connection with you.

My printer says "Replace Toner" but I just changed it. Why?

Brother laser printers use two parts: a toner cartridge and a drum unit. The message can come from either one, and a toner cartridge that is not fully seated can also trigger it. We can help you check which message you have and whether the part is seated properly. We cannot supply parts or reset a worn part.

Do I need the Brother Mobile Connect app?

Not always. It is a free app from Brother for phones and tablets that can print, scan and check ink or toner levels. On a computer, Brother's own software or your system's built-in printing may be enough. If you are not sure, we will help you decide and walk you through it.

Can you help if my Brother printer will not scan to my computer?

Often, yes. Scanning stops working most often after a new router, a new Wi-Fi password, a new computer or a big system update. We can check the printer's connection and the scanning software on your computer together. Menu names differ by model, so we guide you by voice.
