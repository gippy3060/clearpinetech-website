Try it yourself first

Canon printer offline: what to check

Menus and buttons differ by model, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Look at the printer. Many Canon printers show a Wi-Fi symbol on the screen, or have a Wi-Fi light on the control panel. A symbol or light that is off or blinking for a long time usually means the printer is not connected to your Wi-Fi.
  2. Restart in order. Turn off the printer, router and computer. Turn on the router, wait two minutes, then the printer, then the computer.
  3. Check the printer's network details. On many models you can print or view the network settings from the Settings or Setup menu. It shows the Wi-Fi name the printer is joined to. If it is not your home network, the printer needs to be set up again.
  4. Use the same Wi-Fi. Your computer or phone and the printer must be on the same network. Guest networks, and some extender setups, can block printers.
  5. Set up the Wi-Fi connection again. If you changed your Wi-Fi password or router, the printer needs to be told. Many models have a wireless setup option in the Settings menu, or you can use the Canon PRINT app on your phone. Have your Wi-Fi name and password ready.
  6. Check which printer your computer is using. After a re-install, a computer can end up with two copies of the same printer, one that works and one marked offline. Remove the extra one. In Windows: Settings, Bluetooth & devices, Printers & scanners. On a Mac: System Settings, Printers & Scanners.
  7. Get software only from Canon's own website. If you need a driver or scanning software, type the address yourself. Never download it from a pop-up, an advert or a link in a message.

More general steps are on our printer help page. Have an Epson printer? See independent help with Epson printers. If your Wi-Fi is weak where the printer sits, see Wi-Fi extender and mesh setup help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Canon printers

Different families, different parts. Canon PIXMA and MAXIFY are inkjet printers. Many use two cartridges, one black and one colour, and some models use several single-colour tanks. PIXMA G-series printers have refillable ink tanks instead of cartridges. imageCLASS models are laser printers that use toner. Check the model name on the front of your printer and have it ready when you call.

Support codes. When something goes wrong, many Canon printers show a short code with a letter and numbers. Some are simple (a paper jam, a cartridge not seated). A few mean an internal fault or a full waste ink absorber, which usually needs a repair shop. We cannot clear those remotely, and we will say so.

Third-party ink. A cartridge that is not made by Canon may not be recognised, or may show a warning. We can help you check that it is seated properly, but we cannot make an incompatible cartridge work.

What remote support cannot do

  • Repair hardware faults such as worn paper rollers, a clogged print head, a full waste ink absorber or a broken scanner
  • Supply ink, toner or parts
  • Make a very old printer work with a new operating system if Canon no longer provides a driver

We will tell you honestly if the problem looks like hardware. If we can't fix the problem remotely, there is no charge.

Questions

Canon printer help FAQ

My Canon printer says offline. What should I do?

Turn off the printer, router and computer, then turn on the router first, the printer second and the computer last. Then check the printer's screen or Wi-Fi light to see whether it is connected to your Wi-Fi. If it still says offline, call us and we will check the connection with you.

My Canon printer shows a support code or error number. Can you fix it?

Sometimes. Many codes are simple, such as a paper jam, an ink cartridge that is not seated, or a lost Wi-Fi connection. A few codes, such as a serious internal error or a full waste ink absorber, usually need a repair shop. We will tell you honestly which kind you have, and if we can't fix it remotely, there is no charge.

Do I need the Canon PRINT app?

Not always. It is a free app from Canon for iPhone, iPad and Android that can set up the printer's Wi-Fi, print and scan. On a computer, your system's built-in printing may be enough. If you are not sure, we will help you decide and walk you through it.

Can you help if my Canon printer will not scan to my computer?

Often, yes. Scanning stops working most often after a new router, a new Wi-Fi password, a new computer or a big system update. We can check the printer's connection and the scanning software on your computer together. Menu names differ by model, so we guide you by voice.
