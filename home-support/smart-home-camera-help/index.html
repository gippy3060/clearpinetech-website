Try this first

Camera or doorbell offline: a simple checklist

  1. Look at the lights. Most cameras and doorbells have a small light that shows if they are on and connected. The manual or app help page tells you what each colour means.
  2. Check the battery or power. A flat battery is the most common reason a wireless camera goes offline. Charge it fully before anything else.
  3. Check your Wi-Fi where it is mounted. Stand where the camera is with your phone. If the Wi-Fi is weak or gone, the camera cannot connect either.
  4. Restart in order. Unplug the router for 30 seconds, plug it in and wait a few minutes. Then restart the camera hub, if you have one.
  5. Have your sign-in ready. You need the email and password you used when you first set it up. If you forgot them, only the manufacturer can reset them; we can show you where to start.
  6. Still stuck? Call us. We talk you through it and you stay in control.

If a text, pop-up or caller says your camera has been hacked and tells you to phone a number or pay, do not respond. See virus and scam clean-up.

What remote support can and cannot do

Being honest about limits

Questions

Camera and smart home questions

Can you help set up my Ring doorbell or camera?

Yes. We guide you by voice through charging or wiring it, adding it in the Ring app on your phone, joining your home Wi-Fi and checking the picture. Please have your phone, your Wi-Fi name and password, and your Ring account email ready. You type your own passwords; we never need you to read them out.

My Arlo camera says it is offline. What can you do?

Many Arlo cameras connect through a small base station or hub that plugs into your router with a cable. If the router was changed or restarted, the hub may need to rejoin. We walk you through checking the cable and lights, restarting in the right order and re-syncing the camera, and tell you plainly if the camera or battery looks faulty.

Can you help with a Google Nest camera or doorbell?

Yes. Newer Nest cameras and doorbells are managed in the Google Home app, and older ones in the Nest app. We help you find which one you have, sign in with your Google account, set up the camera and fix Wi-Fi problems. We cannot unlock a Google account for you; only Google can do that.

Will you be able to see my camera pictures?

No. We do not need to see your camera pictures to set it up or fix it, and we do not ask for your camera passwords. If we use screen sharing on your phone, you start it, you see everything we do, and you can end it at any time.

Do I need a paid plan to see recordings?

Often, yes. Several camera makers charge a monthly or yearly plan for saved video history, and what is included depends on the brand, model and when you bought it. We can show you where to check your own plan in the app, but plans are set and billed by the manufacturer, not by us.

Why does my doorbell or camera keep dropping off Wi-Fi?

The usual causes are a weak signal where the camera is mounted, a changed Wi-Fi name or password, or a router that was replaced. Many cameras work best on the 2.4 GHz band, which reaches further, but this varies by model. A Wi-Fi extender can help; see our Wi-Fi extender and mesh setup page.
