What we help with

Problems we check and clean up

Fake virus pop-ups

A full-screen warning, a loud alarm, or a message that will not close. We help you close it safely and check that nothing was installed.

Unwanted software and browser hijacks

A home page you did not choose, extra toolbars, ads everywhere, or a search that goes somewhere strange.

Remote-access software left by a scammer

If you let someone connect to your computer, we look for programs that could let them back in, and remove them.

Securing your accounts

Changing passwords, turning on two-factor sign-in, and checking email forwarding rules for your email, banking and shopping accounts.

Antivirus check-up

Checking that your security software is on and up to date, running a full scan, and removing what it finds.

Reporting what happened

We explain who to tell and how: your bank, and the fraud-reporting centre in your country.

Do this first

If you think a scammer has been in your computer

  1. Stop talking to them. Hang up. Do not call the number on a pop-up, and do not call back.
  2. Disconnect from the internet. Turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable. This cuts the connection.
  3. If you paid or shared card or bank details, call your bank or card company using the number on the back of your card. Ask them to stop or review the payment.
  4. Do not turn off the pop-up by clicking inside it. To close a stuck browser on Windows, press Ctrl + Alt + Delete, choose Task Manager, select the browser and choose End task. On a Mac press Option + Command + Esc, choose the browser and click Force Quit.
  5. Change your passwords from a different device, such as a phone you trust, starting with email and banking.
  6. Report it. In Canada, the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca, 1-888-495-8501). In the USA, ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Then call us and we will check the computer with you. Being scammed can happen to anyone, and there is no need to feel embarrassed.

Questions

Virus and scam questions

A pop-up says my computer has a virus and gives a phone number. Is it real?

No. Real security software and real companies do not put a phone number in a browser warning. Do not call it. Close the browser (on Windows press Ctrl + Alt + Delete, open Task Manager and end the browser; on a Mac press Option + Command + Esc and Force Quit). If it comes back, call us and we will check the computer.

I let someone connect to my computer. What should I do?

Turn off the internet connection or unplug the network cable, then call us. We can check for remote-access software, remove it and help you change passwords. If you paid or shared bank details, call your bank or card company right away and report it to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (Canada) or ReportFraud.ftc.gov (USA).

Can you get my money back?

No. We cannot recover money. Your bank or card company is the right place to start, and you should also report the scam to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre or ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
