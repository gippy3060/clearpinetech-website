Try this first

Weak Wi-Fi in one part of the house: a simple checklist

  1. Test in two places. Stand next to the router and use a phone or tablet, then try the weak room. If it is fine next to the router, the signal is the problem, not your internet plan.
  2. Look at where the router sits. Floors, cupboards, behind a TV or beside a metal object all weaken it. A central, open, higher spot is best.
  3. Restart the router. Unplug it for 30 seconds, plug it back in and wait a few minutes.
  4. Keep the extender halfway. If you add one, place it between the router and the weak room, where it still gets a good signal. Inside the weak room it cannot help.
  5. Note your Wi-Fi name and password. They are often on a sticker on the router. Have them ready before any changes.
  6. Still stuck? Call us. We talk you through it and you stay in control.

Questions

Wi-Fi extender and mesh questions

Do I need a Wi-Fi extender or a mesh system?

An extender is a small, lower-cost box that repeats your Wi-Fi into a weak spot. A mesh system replaces or joins your router with two or more units that work as one network, which usually works better in larger or multi-storey homes. We talk through your home and what you already own, and tell you honestly if you may not need either. We do not sell equipment.

Where should I put the extender?

Halfway between your router and the weak room, in a spot where it still gets a good signal from the router. Putting it inside the dead zone does not work, because it can only repeat a signal it can already receive.

Can you set up an extender or mesh unit I already bought?

Yes. We guide you through plugging it in, joining it to your Wi-Fi and checking the signal in the rooms that matter. Please have the Wi-Fi name and password ready.

Why is my Wi-Fi slow upstairs or in the basement?

Walls, floors, metal and distance weaken the signal, and some routers sit in a corner or a cupboard. Moving the router to a central, open spot often helps before buying anything. If it does not, an extender or mesh unit may. We check which applies.

Will an extender fix my slow internet?

Only if the cause is a weak signal in a room. If the internet plan itself is slow, or your provider has a problem, an extender cannot fix that. We can check with you and tell you which it is.
