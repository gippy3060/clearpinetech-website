Try it yourself first

Bell Wi-Fi slow or not working: what to check

Hubs and apps change from time to time, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Is it one device or all of them? If only one device is slow, restart that device first. If everything is slow, keep going.
  2. Restart the Home Hub. Unplug the Bell hub for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait a few minutes until it finishes starting. If you have Wi-Fi pods or a wireless TV box, give them a few minutes too.
  3. Check for an outage. The Bell app or website can show known problems in your area. An outage is for Bell to fix. Do not pay anyone to "fix" an outage.
  4. Check the cables. Make sure the cable from the wall is pushed firmly into the hub. If you have fibre, don't bend or pull the thin fibre cable; it breaks easily.
  5. Test next to the hub. Stand in the same room and try again. If it is fast there but slow upstairs, the signal is not reaching. Our article Why is my Wi-Fi slow upstairs? explains what helps.
  6. Give the hub space. Keep it out of cupboards and away from the microwave, fish tanks and the TV cabinet. A raised, open, central spot works best.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Bell connections

The Home Hub is two boxes in one. Bell usually supplies one box, called a Home Hub or Giga Hub, that is both the modem (the connection to the street) and the Wi-Fi router. Restarting it fixes many problems, but a fault on the line is for Bell.

Fibre homes may have an extra box. In some homes with Bell fibre, the fibre cable first goes into a small separate box on the wall before the hub. Leave it plugged in. If its lights look wrong, that is a question for Bell.

Wi-Fi pods and TV boxes lean on the hub. Bell Wi-Fi pods and wireless Fibe TV boxes are built to work with the Bell hub. That is why replacing the hub with your own router is not always a good idea, and why your own router usually goes behind the hub instead.

Your account is yours. We never ask for your Bell account password. We cannot change your plan, bill or service. If a screen asks for your Bell sign-in, you type it yourself.

Questions

Bell Wi-Fi help FAQ

My Bell Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Restart the Bell Home Hub: unplug its power for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait a few minutes until it finishes starting. Then test close to it. If the Wi-Fi is fine close by but weak in other rooms, the signal is not reaching, and Bell Wi-Fi pods, a mesh system or an extender may help. If the internet is slow even with a cable plugged into the hub, the service itself may be the problem, and Bell would need to check the line.

Where do I find my Bell Wi-Fi name and password?

If nobody has changed them, the Wi-Fi name and password are usually printed on a card or sticker that came with the Bell Home Hub, often on the hub itself. Some hubs can also show them on a small screen or through the Bell app. If they were changed and you have forgotten them, we can help you find or reset them and write them down clearly.

My Fibe TV box says it has lost its connection. Is that the Wi-Fi?

It can be. Some Fibe TV boxes connect to the Home Hub without a cable. If the hub was moved, restarted or replaced, or the Wi-Fi settings were changed, a wireless TV box can lose its link. Restarting the hub and then the TV box often helps. If TV still does not work, Bell supports its own TV service, so contact Bell directly.

I changed my Bell Wi-Fi password and now nothing connects. What now?

That is normal. Every device that used the old password, such as phones, tablets, smart TVs, printers, cameras and smart speakers, needs to be connected again with the new one. We can go through your devices one at a time with you.

Can I use my own router or mesh system with Bell?

Often yes, but it is less simple than with some providers. Bell hubs do not always offer a plain bridge mode, so your own router is usually connected behind the hub, and Fibe TV may need the Bell hub to stay in place. Check with Bell about your plan first. We can then help you set up your own router and turn off the second Wi-Fi so your devices don't get confused.
