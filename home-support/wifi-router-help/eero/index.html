Try it yourself first

Eero Wi-Fi slow or not working: what to check

The eero app changes from time to time, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Is it one device or all of them? If only one phone or computer is slow, restart that device first.
  2. Find the main eero. One eero, called the gateway, plugs into your modem with a cable. The others connect to it without wires. Make sure the gateway's cable is pushed in firmly at both ends.
  3. Restart in order. Unplug the modem and every eero for 30 seconds. Plug in the modem and wait a few minutes. Then plug in the gateway eero, and then the others.
  4. Look at the eero app. The app shows whether your network is online and whether each eero is connected. If the app says the internet is down, the problem is usually the modem or your internet provider, not the eero.
  5. Check the light. A steady white light usually means all is well. A red light on the gateway usually means it cannot reach the internet. Check the modem and its cables first.
  6. Move the extra eeros. Put each one about halfway between the gateway and the weak room, up off the floor and out in the open, not inside a cabinet or behind the TV.
  7. Test with a cable. If a computer plugged into the modem with a cable is also slow, the cause is likely your internet service. Your provider would need to check the line.
  8. Be careful with resetting. A full reset erases your network and means setting it up again in the app. Call us first if you are not sure.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page, and in our article on why Wi-Fi is slow upstairs.

Good to know

Things that are specific to eero

Everything happens in the app. Unlike most routers, eero has no settings page you open on a computer. Setup, Wi-Fi name and password changes, and checking which devices are connected are all done in the eero app on a phone or tablet. If your phone is new, you will need to install the app and sign in again.

Sign-in codes. The eero app signs you in with a one-time code sent by text or email. Keep the phone number and email on the account up to date. Never read that code to anyone who contacts you. We will never ask for it; you type it yourself.

Eero is owned by Amazon. You may be offered the option to link your eero to an Amazon account. That is your choice. Your Wi-Fi works either way.

Behind your provider's modem. If your modem from Rogers, Bell, Telus, Xfinity, Spectrum or another provider also has its own Wi-Fi, adding an eero can create two networks. We help set it up so you have one network that works well, using the option that fits your equipment.

Eero from your internet provider. Some internet providers supply eero units with their service. If yours came from your provider, some settings may be managed by them, and replacements go through them.

Questions

Eero help FAQ

My eero Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Unplug the modem and all your eero units for 30 seconds. Plug in the modem first and wait a few minutes, then plug in the eero that is connected to the modem, then the others. Open the eero app to see whether every unit is online. If Wi-Fi is fine near the main eero but weak in other rooms, an eero may be too far away. If a computer on a cable is also slow, the cause may be your internet service.

How do I set up a new eero?

Eero is set up only in the eero app on a phone or tablet. There is no settings page on a computer. You make or sign in to an eero account, plug the first eero into your modem with the cable, and the app guides you through adding each extra eero. We can walk you through it, one step at a time.

I can't sign in to the eero app. What now?

The eero app signs you in with a code sent to your phone number or email. If you changed your phone number or email, the code goes to the old one. Recovering the account is done through eero itself. A factory reset of the eero units can also let you set them up under a new account. We will explain the choices before anything is erased.

Someone asked me for my eero sign-in code. Is that normal?

No. Never read a sign-in code to anyone who calls, texts or emails you. That code gives control of your Wi-Fi. A real support person helping you does not need it. If someone asked for it, hang up and call us or eero directly using a number you found yourself.

Do I need eero Plus?

No. Eero Plus is an optional paid subscription with extra features. Your Wi-Fi works without it. We can explain what it includes so you can decide for yourself, and we never sell subscriptions.
