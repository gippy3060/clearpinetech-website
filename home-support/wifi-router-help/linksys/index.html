Try it yourself first

Linksys Wi-Fi slow or not working: what to check

Menus and lights differ by model, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Is it one device or all of them? If only one is slow, restart that device first.
  2. Find the main unit. On a Velop system, the main node is the one plugged into your modem with a cable. Problems on the main node affect the whole home.
  3. Check the cable to the modem. Make sure it is pushed in firmly at both ends, and on the Linksys end it is in the internet port (often marked Internet or with a globe symbol, and sometimes a different colour).
  4. Restart in order. Unplug the modem and the Linksys for 30 seconds. Plug in the modem and wait a few minutes. Then plug in the Linksys router or main node, wait for its light to settle, and then plug in the other nodes.
  5. Look at the lights. On most Velop nodes, steady blue means fine. Red usually means that node lost its connection. Yellow usually means it is too far from the others.
  6. Move the nodes. Put the main unit in an open, raised spot, not in a cupboard or on the floor. Place each extra node about halfway between the main unit and the weak room, not inside the weak room.
  7. Test with a cable. If a computer plugged straight into the Linksys is also slow, the cause may be your internet service. Your internet provider would need to check the line.
  8. Be careful with resetting. The small reset hole erases the Wi-Fi name, password and all settings. Only use it if you are ready to set everything up again, or call us first.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Linksys

Velop or a single router? Velop is Linksys's mesh system of several matching units, called nodes. Linksys also makes single routers. Most newer models are set up in the Linksys app on your phone; many single routers also have a settings page you open in a web browser. We start by checking which one you have.

Two different passwords. Your Linksys account (an email and password for the app) is not the same as your Wi-Fi password, and some routers have a third password for their settings page. Write each one down and keep it somewhere safe. We never ask you to tell us your passwords; you type them yourself.

Behind your provider's equipment. If your internet provider gave you a modem with its own Wi-Fi, such as an Xfinity, Spectrum, Rogers or Bell box, adding a Linksys behind it can create two networks. We can help set it up so you have one network that works well. Our pages for Xfinity, Spectrum, Rogers and Bell explain more.

Older models. Linksys has made routers for many years, and some older ones no longer get updates. They may still work, but they will not get new security fixes. We will check yours and tell you plainly.

Questions

Linksys help FAQ

My Linksys Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Restart the modem and then the Linksys: unplug both for 30 seconds, plug the modem in first, wait a few minutes, then plug in the Linksys router or main Velop node, and after that any other nodes. Then test close to the main unit. If Wi-Fi is fine close by but weak in other rooms, the signal is not reaching, and moving a node or adding one may help. If a computer on a cable is also slow, the cause may be your internet service.

What does a red or yellow light on my Velop node mean?

On most Velop nodes, a steady blue light means everything is fine. Red usually means the node has lost its connection: on the main node, to the internet; on another node, to the main one. Yellow usually means a node is connected but too far away from the others, so moving it a little closer often helps. Lights can differ on some models, so check the guide for yours or call us.

I can't sign in to the Linksys app. What now?

The Linksys app uses a Linksys account made with your email address. Make sure you are using the same email that set up the system, and use the app's own password reset if you have forgotten the password. Many Linksys routers also have a separate router password for their settings page. We can help you work out which one you need. We never ask you to tell us your passwords; you type them yourself.

Is my old Linksys router still safe to use?

Older routers can stop receiving updates from the maker. A router that no longer gets updates may still work, but it will not get security fixes. We can help you check your model and update it if an update is available. If it is no longer supported, we will tell you honestly; whether to replace it is your decision.
