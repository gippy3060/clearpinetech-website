Try it yourself first

Netgear Wi-Fi slow or not working: what to check

Menus differ by model, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Is it one device or all of them? If only one is slow, restart that device first.
  2. Check the cable to the modem. The Netgear router, or the main Orbi, plugs into the modem with an Ethernet cable. Make sure it is pushed in at both ends and in the correct port (usually the one marked Internet).
  3. Restart in order. Unplug the modem and the Netgear for 30 seconds. Plug in the modem, wait a few minutes, then plug in the Netgear (the main Orbi first, then the satellites).
  4. Open the app. The Orbi or Nighthawk app shows whether the router is connected to the internet, and which devices are using the Wi-Fi.
  5. Move the units. Put the router or main Orbi in an open, raised, central spot. Place each satellite about halfway between the main unit and the weak room, not in the weak room itself.
  6. Test with a cable. If a computer on a cable is also slow, the cause may be your internet service, not the Netgear. Your internet provider would need to check the line.
  7. Be careful with resetting. A factory reset erases the Wi-Fi name, password and all settings. Only do it if you are ready to set it up again, or call us first.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Netgear

Nighthawk or Orbi? Nighthawk is Netgear's name for its single routers. Orbi is its mesh system, with a main router and one or more satellites. They are set up differently, so we start by checking which one you have.

Two passwords. A Netgear router has a Wi-Fi password, which your devices use, and an admin password for its settings page (routerlogin.net). They can be different. Keep both written down somewhere safe. We never ask you to tell us your passwords; you type them yourself.

Optional paid extras. Netgear's apps may offer paid security or parental-control subscriptions. They are optional, and the router's basic Wi-Fi works without them. We will not sign you up for anything.

Behind your provider's equipment. If your internet provider gave you a modem with its own Wi-Fi, adding a Netgear behind it can create two networks. We can help set it up so you have just one that works well.

Questions

Netgear help FAQ

My Netgear Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Restart the modem and then the Netgear router: unplug both for 30 seconds, plug the modem in first, wait a few minutes, then plug in the router. Move closer and test again. If Wi-Fi is fine close by but weak in other rooms, the signal is not reaching, and a different position or an Orbi satellite may help. If the internet is slow even with a cable plugged in, the cause may be your internet service.

How do I set up a new Netgear router or Orbi?

The router connects to your modem with an Ethernet cable. Orbi systems are set up in the Orbi app on your phone, starting with the main router that plugs into the modem and then adding each satellite. Nighthawk routers can be set up in the Nighthawk app or on a web page on your computer. We can walk you through it step by step.

How do I open my Netgear router's settings page?

On a device connected to the router, type routerlogin.net into your web browser's address bar. It asks for the router's admin password, which is not always the same as your Wi-Fi password. If you changed it and forgot it, the router may need a factory reset, which erases its settings and means setting it up again. We will tell you before doing that, and we cannot bypass anything for you.

Should I update my Netgear router?

Updates can fix problems and improve security. Many models can check for updates in the Orbi or Nighthawk app or on the router's settings page. We can show you how, and we will not install anything without asking you first.
