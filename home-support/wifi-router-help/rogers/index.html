Try it yourself first

Rogers Wi-Fi slow or not working: what to check

Gateways and apps change from time to time, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Is it one device or all of them? If only one device is slow, restart that device first. If everything is slow, keep going.
  2. Restart the gateway. Unplug the Rogers gateway for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait a few minutes until it finishes starting. If you have pods, give them a few minutes too.
  3. Check for an outage. The Rogers app or website can show known outages in your area. An outage is for Rogers to fix. Do not pay anyone to "fix" an outage.
  4. Test next to the gateway. Stand in the same room and try again. If it is fast there but slow upstairs, the signal is not reaching. Our article Why is my Wi-Fi slow upstairs? explains what helps.
  5. Give the gateway space. Keep it out of cupboards and away from the microwave, fish tanks and the TV cabinet. A raised, open, central spot works best.
  6. Look for the sticker. If a device asks for the password and you don't know it, check the sticker on the gateway. It only works if nobody has changed the password since.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Rogers connections

The gateway is two boxes in one. Rogers usually supplies one box that is both the modem (the connection to the street) and the Wi-Fi router. Restarting it fixes many problems, but a fault on the line is for Rogers.

Pods are Rogers' own mesh. Some plans include small Wi-Fi pods that plug into the wall to carry the signal further. They work with the Rogers gateway, and a pod placed too far away gives a weak signal of its own. We help you find a better spot.

Former Shaw customers. Since Shaw joined Rogers, many homes in British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba are getting Rogers equipment and apps. Old Shaw instructions may not match the new box.

Questions

Rogers Wi-Fi help FAQ

My Rogers Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Restart the Rogers gateway: unplug its power for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait a few minutes until it finishes starting. Then test close to it. If the Wi-Fi is fine close by but weak in other rooms, the signal is not reaching, and Rogers pods, a mesh system or an extender may help. If the internet is slow even with a cable plugged into the gateway, the service itself may be the problem, and Rogers would need to check the line.

Where do I find my Rogers Wi-Fi name and password?

If nobody has changed them, the Wi-Fi name and password are usually printed on a sticker on the Rogers gateway, often on the back or bottom. If they were changed, you can usually see or change them in the Rogers app for your home internet. We can help you find them and write them down clearly.

I changed my Rogers Wi-Fi password and now nothing connects. What now?

That is normal. Every device that used the old password, such as phones, tablets, smart TVs, printers, cameras and smart speakers, needs to be connected again with the new one. We can go through your devices one at a time with you.

I was a Shaw customer. Does this page apply to me?

Shaw is now part of Rogers, so many former Shaw customers in Western Canada are moving to Rogers equipment and apps. The equipment in your home may still be a Shaw model, or it may be a newer Rogers one. We start by checking which one you have, because the steps differ.

Can I use my own router or mesh system with Rogers?

Often yes. The usual way is to set the Rogers gateway to bridge mode so your own router takes over the Wi-Fi. This is not always possible with every plan or with Rogers TV equipment, so check with Rogers first. We can help you set up and connect your own router once you know what your plan allows.
