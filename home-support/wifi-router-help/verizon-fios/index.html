Try it yourself first

Verizon Fios Wi-Fi slow or not working: what to check

Routers and lights differ by model, so use these as a guide. If they don't help, call us.

  1. Check the whole house. Is the problem on one device, or on all of them? If only one device is slow, restart that device first.
  2. Restart the Fios router the safe way. Unplug its power cord, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in. Give it a few minutes to start up fully before you test.
  3. Don't press the reset hole. The small recessed button can put the router back to factory settings and erase a Wi-Fi name and password you chose. A power restart is enough.
  4. Leave the fiber box alone. The fiber line ends at a box on a wall, often in the garage, basement or a closet. Don't open it or bend its cables. Check that the cable from that box to your router is plugged in firmly at both ends.
  5. Check for an outage. The My Fios app or your Verizon account online may show a known problem in your area. An outage is for Verizon to fix. Don't pay anyone to "fix" it.
  6. Stand next to the router and test. If Wi-Fi is fine there but weak upstairs or at the back of the house, the signal is not reaching. Read why Wi-Fi is often slow upstairs.
  7. Give the router a good spot. Open, raised and central. Not in a cabinet, behind the TV, on the floor or next to a microwave or fish tank.
  8. Keep the sticker safe. The label on the Fios router shows the Wi-Fi name and password, and a separate password for the router's settings. Take a photo of it, and don't read those codes to anyone who calls you.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Verizon Fios

Two boxes, not one. On most Fios lines, the fiber ends at a box on a wall (Verizon calls it an ONT), often in the garage, basement or a closet. A cable runs from there to the Fios router, which makes your Wi-Fi. When people say "the router", they mean the second box, the one you can restart yourself.

A beeping box is usually the battery backup. Some homes have a battery unit next to the fiber box. It keeps a Fios home phone working in a power outage. If it beeps, it usually needs a new battery. It doesn't affect your Wi-Fi, and it is not a sign of a virus or a hacker.

Your own router may replace the Fios router, but check the TV first. On many Fios connections, your own router can plug into the fiber box in place of the Fios router. But Fios TV boxes may rely on the Fios router for the guide and on-demand shows. In that case the Fios router stays, and your own system is added behind it. We check what you have before changing anything.

5G Home Internet is different. If your Verizon internet comes over the cellular network, the equipment and steps are different. Tell us when you call, and we will be honest if it's outside what we can help with.

Your account is yours. We never ask for your Verizon account password. We can't change your plan, bill or service. If a screen asks for your Verizon sign-in, you type it yourself.

Questions

Verizon Fios Wi-Fi help FAQ

My Verizon Fios Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Restart the Fios router: unplug its power cord, wait 30 seconds, plug it back in and give it a few minutes to start up fully. Then stand near the router and test again. If the Wi-Fi is fine close by but weak in other rooms, the signal is not reaching, and moving the router or adding an extender may help. If the internet is slow even next to the router or with a cable plugged in, the problem may be with the service, and Verizon would need to check it.

Where do I find my Verizon Fios Wi-Fi password?

If you never changed it, the Wi-Fi name and password are printed on a sticker on the Fios router. The same sticker usually also shows a separate password for the router's settings. If you changed the Wi-Fi password, you can usually see or change it in the My Fios app or your Verizon account online. We can walk you through it on a call.

What is the box in my garage, basement or closet?

In most Fios homes the fiber line ends at a box on a wall, often in the garage, basement or a utility closet. Verizon calls it an ONT. It turns the fiber signal into internet for your router. Don't unplug it or open it. Some homes also have a battery backup unit next to it. If that unit beeps, it is usually about its battery, which keeps a Fios home phone working in a power outage. It is not a Wi-Fi problem.

Can I use my own router or mesh system with Verizon Fios?

Often yes. On many Fios connections, your own router can connect to the fiber box in place of the Fios router. But Fios TV boxes may need the Fios router for the guide and on-demand shows, so some homes keep the Fios router and add their own system behind it. We help you choose the setup that keeps everything working. Verizon 5G Home Internet uses different equipment, so tell us if you have that service.
