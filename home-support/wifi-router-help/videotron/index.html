Try it yourself first

Videotron Wi-Fi slow or not working: what to check

Equipment and apps change from time to time, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Is it one device or all of them? If only one device is slow, restart that device first. If everything is slow, keep going.
  2. Restart the gateway. Unplug the Videotron gateway for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait a few minutes until it finishes starting. If you have Wi-Fi pods, give them a few minutes too. The TV may pause while the gateway restarts.
  3. Leave the cable screwed in. The round cable on the back of the gateway brings the internet in. Do not unscrew it. If it looks loose or damaged, that is for Videotron.
  4. Check for an outage. The Videotron website or app can show known service problems in your area. An outage is for Videotron to fix. Do not pay anyone to "fix" an outage.
  5. Test next to the gateway. Stand in the same room and try again. If it is fast there but slow upstairs, the signal is not reaching. Our article Why is my Wi-Fi slow upstairs? explains what helps.
  6. Give the gateway space. Keep it out of cupboards and away from the microwave, fish tanks and the TV cabinet. A raised, open, central spot works best.
  7. Look for the sticker. If a device asks for the password and you don't know it, check the sticker on the gateway. It only works if nobody has changed the password since.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page. Other Canadian providers: Rogers Wi-Fi help, Telus Wi-Fi help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Videotron connections

One box makes your Wi-Fi. With Helix, Videotron usually supplies one gateway that is both the modem and the Wi-Fi router. Restarting it fixes many problems, but a fault on the line is for Videotron.

Helix TV goes through the gateway. The Helix TV boxes get their connection from the gateway. If the gateway restarts, the TV stops for a few minutes. If you swap the gateway or add your own router, the TV may stop working. Ask Videotron before you change anything.

Wi-Fi pods carry the signal further. Some plans include Wi-Fi pods that plug into the wall in far rooms. A pod placed too far from the gateway gets a weak signal of its own. We help you find a better spot.

Older homes, thick walls. Brick, plaster and stone walls, common in older Montreal and Quebec City homes, block Wi-Fi more than drywall does. Placement matters even more there.

Your account is yours. We never ask for your Videotron account password. We cannot change your plan, bill or service. If a screen asks for your Videotron sign-in, you type it yourself.

Questions

Videotron Wi-Fi help FAQ

My Videotron Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Restart the Videotron Helix gateway: unplug its power for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait a few minutes until it finishes starting. Then test close to it. If the Wi-Fi is fine close by but weak in other rooms, the signal is not reaching, and Videotron's Wi-Fi pods, a mesh system or an extender may help. If the internet is slow even with a cable plugged into the gateway, the service itself may be the problem, and Videotron would need to check the line.

Where do I find my Videotron Wi-Fi name and password?

If nobody has changed them, the Wi-Fi name and password are usually printed on a sticker on the Videotron gateway. If they were changed, you can usually see or change them in the Helix app or in your Videotron customer account. We can help you find them and write them down clearly.

Should I restart the TV box or the gateway?

If the Wi-Fi is the problem, restart the gateway, the box that makes the Wi-Fi. With Helix, the TV boxes get their connection through the gateway, so the TV may stop for a few minutes while the gateway restarts. That is normal. Leave the round coaxial cable on the back of the gateway screwed in.

I changed my Videotron Wi-Fi password and now nothing connects. What now?

That is normal. Every device that used the old password, such as phones, tablets, smart TVs, printers, cameras and smart speakers, needs to be connected again with the new one. We can go through your devices one at a time with you.

Can I use my own router or mesh system with Videotron?

Sometimes, but Helix TV relies on the Videotron gateway, and changing how it is set up can stop the TV from working. Check with Videotron what your plan allows before changing anything. We can help you set up and connect your own router once you know.
