Try it yourself first

Xfinity Wi-Fi slow or not working: what to check

Equipment and apps differ, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Check the lights. Look at the lights on your gateway. Colours and patterns differ by model. If your provider's app or website shows an outage in your area, wait it out. That is not something you or we can fix.
  2. Restart the gateway. Unplug its power cord, wait 30 seconds, plug it back in, and give it a few minutes. Do not press a small reset button; that can erase your Wi-Fi name and password.
  3. Test close to the gateway. Stand in the same room and test one device. Fast there but slow elsewhere means a range problem.
  4. Check the Wi-Fi name. Many gateways give out one Wi-Fi name for both the faster and the longer-range bands. Some devices pick badly. Restarting just that device often helps.
  5. Move the gateway. Keep it up off the floor, in the open, near the middle of the home. Cupboards, TV cabinets and basements weaken the signal.
  6. Forget and rejoin. On the device that fails, tell it to forget the Wi-Fi network and join again with the password.
  7. Count the devices. A video call on one device and a download on another can slow everything. Pause big downloads and test again.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page. For rooms the signal can't reach, see Wi-Fi extender and mesh setup help.

Good to know

Things that are specific to Xfinity gateways

Modem and router in one. Many homes have one box that is both the modem and the Wi-Fi router, often called a gateway. Adding your own router can mean two devices both doing the Wi-Fi, which can cause odd problems. We can help you sort out the set-up.

The provider's app. Many gateways can be managed from the provider's phone app, including the Wi-Fi name and password. We can help you install it and sign in.

What only your provider can do. Outages, billing, plan speeds, activating new equipment and sending a technician must be done with your provider. We help with everything on your side of the wall.

Remote tools. We connect to a Windows computer with Quick Assist, or to a Mac with LogMeIn. You start it, you see everything we do, and you can end it at any time. Gateways and TVs are guided by voice.

Questions

Xfinity Wi-Fi help FAQ

My Xfinity Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Restart the gateway: unplug its power cord, wait 30 seconds and plug it back in, then give it a few minutes to start up. Move it out of cupboards and away from the floor if you can. Test the speed on a device close to it. If it is fast close up but slow far away, the problem is Wi-Fi range, not your plan. Call us and we will go through it with you.

How do I find or change my Xfinity Wi-Fi name and password?

If you use the provider's app, the Wi-Fi name and password can usually be seen and changed there. The label on the gateway may also show the original name and password. If you changed them and forgot, call us and we will help you find your way back in. Anything you change means each device must be reconnected with the new password.

Can I use my own router or mesh system with Xfinity?

Often yes. Many people keep the provider's modem and add their own router or mesh system, or use their own modem. The exact steps depend on your equipment and plan. We can help set up a mesh system or extender so your whole home is covered. We do not sell equipment, and we cannot change your plan or account with your provider.

Can you fix an Xfinity outage or a billing problem?

No. Outages, billing, plan changes and account changes can only be handled by your internet provider. We can help you check whether the problem is inside your home, such as your Wi-Fi or a device, or outside it.
