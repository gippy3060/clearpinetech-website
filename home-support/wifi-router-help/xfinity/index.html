Try it yourself first

Xfinity Wi-Fi slow or dropping: what to check

Gateway models and app screens differ, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Find your gateway. For most homes it is one box that is both the modem and the Wi-Fi router. Make sure it is plugged in and the cables are firm.
  2. Test next to it. Stand in the same room as the gateway and use a phone or laptop. If it is fast there, your internet is working and the issue is Wi-Fi reach.
  3. Try a cable. If you can, plug one computer into the gateway with an Ethernet cable. If that is also slow or offline, the cause may be your service, not your Wi-Fi.
  4. Restart the gateway. Unplug it, wait 30 seconds, plug it back in and wait a few minutes. Do not press a small recessed reset button unless asked, as it can erase your Wi-Fi name and password.
  5. Move it up and out. Wi-Fi spreads best from a central, high, open spot. Avoid closets, cabinets, and next to a microwave or the TV.
  6. Check the Xfinity app. Many gateways show their status and connected devices there. An outage notice means waiting, not fixing.
  7. Forget and rejoin. On a device that will not connect, choose "Forget this network" and join again with the password.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page. For rooms the signal does not reach, see Wi-Fi extender and mesh setup help.

Questions

Xfinity Wi-Fi help FAQ

My Xfinity Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Restart the Xfinity gateway (the box that combines the modem and Wi-Fi router) by unplugging it for 30 seconds, then plugging it back in and waiting a few minutes. Then test Wi-Fi in the same room as the gateway. If it is fast there but slow upstairs or at the far end of the house, the problem is usually distance or walls, not your plan. Call us and we will look at it with you.

How do I change my Xfinity Wi-Fi name or password?

On most Xfinity gateways you can do this in the Xfinity app on your phone, signed in to your Xfinity account. The steps vary a little by gateway and app version. We can walk you through it and then reconnect your devices, one at a time, at your pace.

Can I use my own router or mesh system with Xfinity?

Often yes. Many people use their own modem, or their own router or mesh system together with the Xfinity gateway. How it is done depends on your equipment and your plan, so we check first. We do not sell equipment and we cannot change your account or plan.

Is my internet down, or is it my Wi-Fi?

If no device in the house can get online, even one plugged in with a cable, the cause may be an outage or a line problem. We cannot fix those remotely. Check Xfinity's outage page or app, or contact Xfinity. If the cable-connected device works but Wi-Fi does not, call us.
