Try it yourself first

Xfinity Wi-Fi slow or not working: what to check

Lights and menus differ by gateway model, so use these as a guide. If they do not help, call us.

  1. Check the whole house. Is the problem on one device, or all of them? If only one device is slow, restart that device first.
  2. Restart the gateway. Unplug it for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait a few minutes until the lights settle.
  3. Test with a cable. If you can, plug a computer into the gateway with an Ethernet cable. If the cable is fast but Wi-Fi is slow, the problem is the Wi-Fi signal in your home. If both are slow, the service may be the cause.
  4. Look at the Xfinity app. It may show whether there is a known outage in your area, and which devices are connected.
  5. Check for a service problem. If lights on the gateway show a fault, or the app reports an outage, that is for Xfinity to fix. Do not pay anyone to "fix" an outage on the provider's side.
  6. Move closer, then move the gateway. Put it in an open, raised, central spot, away from microwaves, cordless phone bases and thick walls. Do not hide it in a cupboard.
  7. Count your devices. Old tablets, smart plugs and cameras all use the Wi-Fi. Turning off ones you no longer use can help.

More general steps are on our Wi-Fi and router help page.

Questions

Xfinity Wi-Fi help FAQ

My Xfinity Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping. What can I try?

Restart the gateway: unplug it for 30 seconds, plug it back in and wait a few minutes until the lights settle. Move closer to it and test again. If the Wi-Fi is fine close by but weak in other rooms, the signal is not reaching, and a mesh system or extender may help. If the internet is slow even with a cable plugged in, the problem may be with the service itself, and Xfinity would need to check the line.

How do I change my Xfinity Wi-Fi name or password?

Many customers can do this in the Xfinity app on their phone, or by signing in to their Xfinity account. We can walk you through it. After you change the password, every device, including printers, TVs and cameras, must be reconnected with the new one. We can help with that too.

Can I use my own router or mesh system with Xfinity?

In many cases yes, if you have your own compatible modem or put the Xfinity gateway into a mode that lets your router take over. What works depends on your equipment and plan, so check with Xfinity first. We can help you set up and connect your own router once you know what your plan allows.

My Xfinity gateway has a light that is not white. What does that mean?

Light colours and patterns differ between gateway models, so we will not guess. Xfinity publishes a guide for each model on its website. If you tell us the model, we can look at it with you. A light that points to a line or service fault needs Xfinity to fix it, and we will tell you so honestly.
