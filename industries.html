We know your environment before we arrive

Generic IT support treats every business the same. A clinic is not a law office is not a government department — the equipment, the obligations and the consequences of downtime are all different.

Core specialisation

Optometrist clinic support

OptometryOptical dispensaries DentalMedical practices Multi-site

Optometry practices are where our experience runs deepest. Our founder has built complete optometry clinic infrastructure on engagements up to $40,000 — and the pattern is consistent: the technology is not especially exotic, but the constraints are unforgiving. The same approach extends to dental and medical practices.

What makes clinic IT different

  • Patient data is regulated. Personal health information falls under PIPEDA and provincial privacy legislation. Access control, encryption, retention and breach response are obligations, not best practices.
  • Downtime has a face. When the system is down, patients are physically in the waiting room. Change windows go outside clinic hours; every change has a rollback plan.
  • Diagnostic equipment is fussy. Imaging and testing devices often run vendor software with specific requirements and long replacement cycles. They have to integrate with practice management software cleanly.
  • Staff are not IT people. Optometrists and opticians should not be troubleshooting a printer between appointments. Support has to be reachable and fast.
  • Budgets are finite and real. Clinics do not carry contingency funds. Scope creep is not a negotiation, it is a broken commitment.

What we deliver for clinics

  • Complete new-clinic builds — server, workstations, network, cabling, firewall, backup
  • Practice management software environment setup and diagnostic equipment integration
  • Network segmentation and firewall configuration appropriate to health information
  • Backup and recovery designed to a stated objective, then tested
  • Managed support with response times aligned to clinic hours
  • Hardware lifecycle planning so replacements are budgeted, not emergencies
  • Google Ads and local SEO for practices that want patient acquisition handled too

Public sector

Clearpine Technologies Inc. is federally incorporated under the Canada Business Corporations Act and registered extraprovincially in British Columbia, with verifiable corporate details available for vendor onboarding and due diligence.

The disciplines that clinic work demands — protecting personal information, documenting everything, delivering to fixed scope, and planning changes around people who cannot absorb an outage — are the same ones public sector delivery is evaluated on.

How we engage

  • Direct bidding on federal, provincial and municipal opportunities, with proposals structured against the stated evaluation grid and mandatory requirements addressed explicitly
  • Subcontracting to prime contractors as specialist capacity on defined workstreams, working to the prime's standards, reporting cadence and security requirements
  • Security screening — personnel available for federal screening at the level a contract requires
  • Insurance — commercial general liability, professional liability and cyber coverage, with certificates provided during evaluation

We are candid about where we stand: Clearpine is a new corporation, and we say so rather than dressing it up. What we bring is hands-on infrastructure and security delivery experience, documented process, and the flexibility of a small firm that answers the phone.
