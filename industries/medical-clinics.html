When the system is down in a clinic, the cost is immediate: appointments back up, charts are unavailable and staff improvise on paper. Clearpine Technologies is an Abbotsford managed IT provider, and clinic infrastructure is where our experience runs deepest. See our clinic build case study and optometry clinic experience.

What clinics typically need

  • A stable environment for your EMR or practice software. Whether you run OSCAR Pro, Accuro, TELUS PS Suite, Med Access or dental and optometry practice-management software, the workstations, server (if any), network and permissions around it need to be patched, documented and fast. We work alongside your software vendor rather than replacing their support.
  • Privacy safeguards you can show. Unique user accounts, role-based access, encryption of laptops and servers, screen locks and audit-friendly logging. These are the technical side of your obligations under PIPA; see PIPEDA and clinic IT for how we translate them.
  • Backups that restore. Backup to a stated recovery objective, including the EMR database and imaging, with restores tested rather than assumed. See why backups quietly fail.
  • Ransomware readiness. Patching, endpoint protection, multi-factor authentication, network segmentation and an offline or immutable backup copy, plus a plain-language plan for the first hour of an incident. See five security gaps we see in clinics.
  • Secure fax and referral workflow. Many clinics still depend on fax. We help review whether e-fax or secure messaging fits your workflow and how those documents are stored and accessed.
  • Imaging and diagnostic equipment. Vendor software with specific requirements and long replacement cycles has to integrate cleanly without exposing the rest of your network.
  • Front-desk reliability. Printers, card terminals, phones and Wi-Fi that work, with support that responds in clinic hours so your staff are not troubleshooting between patients.

Questions

Clinic IT: common questions

Can you support our EMR, such as OSCAR Pro, Accuro or TELUS PS Suite?

We support the servers or workstations, network, backup, security and user accounts around your EMR or practice-management software, and we coordinate with the vendor on application-level issues. We do not replace the vendor's own support, and where your EMR is hosted by the vendor, we work with their requirements.

What privacy law applies to my clinic's IT in BC?

Private-sector health practices in BC are generally subject to the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), and some organizations may also be subject to other legislation depending on how they are funded and structured. Your regulatory college and your own counsel can confirm what applies to you. We help put the technical safeguards in place and document them.

Do you work with dental offices, physiotherapists and other allied-health practices?

Yes. The IT needs of a dental office, optometry clinic, physiotherapy or chiropractic practice, counselling office or veterinary clinic are similar in shape: regulated personal information, imaging or practice software, front-desk reliability and a need for backups that actually restore. We scope support to the practice.

Where do you provide on-site service for clinics?

From our Abbotsford head office we attend on site across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland, and provide remote support across Canada.
