IT for clinics where the equipment is as busy as the staff

A veterinary clinic is part medical practice, part retail counter and part lab. When the network drops, the X-ray workstation cannot reach patient records, reminders stop and the front desk cannot take payment. Clearpine Technologies is an Abbotsford managed IT provider with experience in clinic infrastructure; see our clinic build case study, and our pages for medical clinics, dental offices and allied health clinics.

What veterinary clinics typically need

  • A network that carries more than office traffic. Practice software, digital X-ray, ultrasound, in-house blood analyzers and monitors often share one network with the front desk. We separate clinical equipment from staff and guest devices and document who can reach what.
  • Reliable practice management software. Whether the system is hosted or runs from a server in the back room, it needs working accounts, individual logins, updates scheduled outside appointments and a tested way back if it fails.
  • Imaging and lab data that is not lost. Radiographs and lab results are large and clinically important. Backups should cover the image archive and equipment workstations, not just the office files, and be test-restored. See EMR backup best practices for BC clinics; the same principles apply.
  • Client data kept private. Client names, addresses, phone numbers, payment details and pet histories are personal information. Encrypted devices, multi-factor authentication and prompt removal of access when staff leave are the basics. See PIPEDA and clinic IT.
  • Payments, phones and printers. Card terminals, VoIP phones, label and receipt printers and reminder systems are small items that stop the day when they fail. We support them during business hours.
  • Equipment in messy places. Treatment rooms, kennel areas and surgery can be hard on computers and Wi-Fi. We plan device placement, cabling and access points with the layout of the building in mind.
  • Mobile and satellite sites. Mobile vets and clinics with more than one location need secure remote access and consistent device setup.
  • Ransomware readiness. Patching, endpoint protection, email filtering and an offline or immutable backup copy. See five security gaps we see in clinics.

Questions

Veterinary clinic IT: common questions

Do you support veterinary clinics and animal hospitals?

Yes. A vet clinic combines a front desk, practice management software, imaging and lab equipment and often a pharmacy, all of which depend on a reliable network and good backups. We scope support to the size of your clinic.

How much does managed IT support cost for a veterinary clinic?

Our managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. It is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, although on-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely. For clinics with fewer than 5 users, we can set up custom billing.

Can you support our practice management software and imaging equipment?

We support the computers, network, accounts and backups that your practice management software, digital X-ray, ultrasound and in-house lab equipment depend on. For application-level or equipment-specific issues, the software or equipment vendor remains the right contact, and we work alongside them.

Do you provide after-hours support for emergency clinics?

After-hours support is available but billed separately from the standard plan, which is delivered remotely during business hours. Contact us to discuss what your clinic needs.

Does BC privacy law apply to a veterinary clinic's client records?

Client contact details and payment information are personal information, and private businesses in BC are generally subject to the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). The College of Veterinarians of BC and your own advisors can confirm what applies to your clinic. We help put technical safeguards in place and document them.

Can you assess our clinic on site?

Yes. Our free IT assessment is free with no obligation, and can be done on site at any location within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely.
