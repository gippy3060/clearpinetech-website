Managed IT Services & Support in Abbotsford, BC

Abbotsford · Local & Fraser Valley IT support

Clearpine is based in Abbotsford, so “local IT support” here means what it should: someone who can be on site the same day, not a call centre reading a script from another province.

We work with Abbotsford clinics, professional offices and general small businesses that need a reliable helpdesk, tested backups, and network security that goes beyond the router's factory settings — without the overhead of a full-time IT hire.

What we cover for Abbotsford businesses

  • Managed IT support — helpdesk, endpoint management, Microsoft 365 administration and proactive monitoring under a written service agreement with defined response times.
  • Cybersecurity — firewall configuration, multi-factor authentication, network segmentation and backup testing, scoped to what a small or mid-sized Abbotsford business actually needs.
  • Hardware deployment — servers, workstations, networking and structured cabling for new offices, relocations and refresh projects.
  • Co-managed IT — backing up an existing in-house IT person with after-hours coverage, a second set of hands for projects, and vacation/sick-day continuity.

Response & travel time

Onsite visits in Abbotsford are typically same-day. We also cover the rest of the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver from the same office.

Who we work with in Abbotsford

Clinics and healthcare offices, professional practices, manufacturing and agriculture-adjacent businesses, and general small-to-mid-sized companies across the city.

Managed IT support pricing in Abbotsford

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Support is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, but on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely. For offices with fewer than 5 users we offer custom billing on the same structure.

Free IT assessment for Abbotsford offices

Our free IT assessment is no-obligation and can be done on site at any location within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely. We review backups, security, Microsoft 365, networking and the day-to-day problems your staff put up with, then tell you plainly what matters and what doesn’t.
