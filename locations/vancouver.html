Managed IT Services & Support in Vancouver, BC

Vancouver · Remote-first support, scheduled onsite

Vancouver has no shortage of managed IT providers, most of them based downtown or in Burnaby. Clearpine works with Vancouver businesses who want the same fixed-scope, fixed-price discipline we run in the Fraser Valley — handled remote-first day to day, with onsite visits scheduled around traffic and travel time rather than promised same-day.

Day-to-day support — helpdesk, Microsoft 365 administration, monitoring and security — runs remotely for the vast majority of issues, the same way it would for any client. Deployments, cabling, and hands-on project work are scheduled onsite visits.

What we cover for Vancouver businesses

  • Managed IT support — helpdesk, endpoint management, Microsoft 365 administration and proactive monitoring under a written service agreement with defined response times.
  • Cybersecurity — firewall configuration, multi-factor authentication, network segmentation and backup testing, scoped to what a small or mid-sized Vancouver business actually needs.
  • Hardware deployment — servers, workstations, networking and structured cabling for new offices, relocations and refresh projects.
  • Co-managed IT — backing up an existing in-house IT person with after-hours coverage, a second set of hands for projects, and vacation/sick-day continuity.

Response & travel time

Vancouver is roughly 45–70 minutes from our Abbotsford office depending on traffic; onsite visits are scheduled rather than same-day, and most support is handled remotely.

Who we work with in Vancouver

Professional services and small businesses looking for a fixed-scope alternative to a large downtown MSP.

Practices and firms in Vancouver can also see our dedicated pages on IT support for medical and dental clinics and IT support for law firms.
