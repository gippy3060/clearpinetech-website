Managed IT services for Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley businesses

Clearpine Technologies is an Abbotsford managed service provider (MSP). We run your help desk, devices, Microsoft 365, security and backups for a flat $95 per user per month.

An IT department without hiring one

Help deskMicrosoft 365 SecurityBackupMonitoring

Most small and mid-sized organizations do not need a full-time IT department. They need someone reliable to answer when something breaks, and someone diligent enough that things break less often. That is managed IT: ongoing, accountable support under a written agreement, instead of calling a technician only after an outage. Read our comparison of managed IT versus an in-house hire and of an independent contractor versus an MSP.

What is included

  • Help desk. Staff can reach us by phone and email, with response and resolution targets set out in the service agreement.
  • Endpoint management. Standardised device builds and patching on a schedule rather than when someone remembers.
  • Microsoft 365 administration. User lifecycle, licensing, mailboxes and security hardening.
  • Security. Multi-factor authentication, endpoint protection, firewall and network design, least-privilege access and offboarding that removes access.
  • Backup verification. Backups tested by performing restores, not just scheduled. See why your backups are probably not working.
  • Monitoring. Disk, service and backup alerts so failures surface before users notice.
  • Asset and licence inventory. Kept current so renewals and end-of-life hardware do not surprise your budget.

What managed IT services cost

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement, delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately. On-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely. Fewer than 5 users? We can set up custom billing. For example budgets, see our cost guides for dental offices, law firms and allied health clinics.

Already have IT staff?

Our co-managed IT arrangement backs up an in-house IT person or team with after-hours coverage, project capacity, security tooling and vacation cover, with boundaries agreed in writing.

Who we work with

We have particular depth in clinics and professional practices that hold personal information: medical clinics, dental offices, allied health clinics, veterinary clinics and law firms. See our case studies.

Managed IT services: common questions

What does a managed IT service provider (MSP) do?

An MSP takes ongoing responsibility for your day-to-day IT: a help desk for staff, patching and device management, Microsoft 365 administration, security controls, backup monitoring and planning, under a written service agreement instead of billing only when something breaks.

How much do managed IT services cost?

Our managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement, delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, although on-site visits are free if the problem cannot be fixed remotely. For fewer than 5 users, we can set up custom billing.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Abbotsford and provide managed IT remotely across Canada. On-site visits and free assessments are available within 300 km of Abbotsford, which covers the Fraser Valley and the Lower Mainland, including Chilliwack, Mission, Langley, Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby, New Westminster, Maple Ridge and North Vancouver.

Can you work with our existing IT person?

Yes. Our co-managed IT arrangement gives an in-house IT person or team backup, such as after-hours coverage, project capacity and vacation cover, with responsibilities agreed in writing.

How do we start?

Book a free IT assessment. It is free with no obligation, can be done on site within 300 km of Abbotsford or remotely, and reviews your security, Microsoft 365, backups and network with a written list of findings.
