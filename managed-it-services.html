Clearpine Technologies is a managed service provider (MSP) based in Abbotsford, serving the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver. We take ongoing responsibility for the systems your staff rely on, so problems are fixed quickly and, with proactive monitoring and patching, happen less often.

What managed IT includes

  • Helpdesk. Staff reach us by phone or email for everyday problems, from lockouts to slow laptops to printer failures.
  • Device management and patching. Standardised builds and updates on a schedule, not when someone remembers.
  • Microsoft 365 administration. New starters and leavers, licences, mailboxes, multi-factor authentication and security settings.
  • Cybersecurity basics done properly. Endpoint protection, email filtering, least-privilege access and a plan for when something goes wrong. See cybersecurity.
  • Backup you have tested. Most failed recoveries are discovered during the recovery. Read why backups quietly fail.
  • Monitoring. Disk, service and backup alerts so failures surface before users notice.
  • Asset and licence inventory. Renewals and end-of-life hardware stop ambushing your budget.

  • After-hours support: billed separately

Managed IT services: common questions

What is a managed service provider (MSP)?

A managed service provider takes ongoing responsibility for your IT: helpdesk, device management, patching, Microsoft 365 administration, backup and monitoring, under a written agreement and for a predictable monthly fee instead of billing only when something breaks.

How much does managed IT cost in the Lower Mainland?

Our managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users on a 1-year agreement. Microsoft 365 licences and after-hours support are billed separately. On-site visits are billed separately too, but are free if we cannot fix the problem remotely.

Is managed IT worth it for a small business?

If your business depends on email, files and a handful of line-of-business apps but does not justify a full-time IT hire, usually yes. See our comparison of managed IT and in-house IT to weigh the trade-offs.

Do you replace our IT person or work alongside them?

Either. Some businesses use us as their whole IT department; others keep an internal person and hand us the monitoring, patching, security and backup work. We scope it to what you need.

Where do you provide managed IT services?

From our Abbotsford head office we serve the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver, including Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Mission, Langley, Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby, New Westminster, Maple Ridge and North Vancouver, with remote support across Canada.

What happens when we want to start?

We begin with a free, no-obligation IT assessment, on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford for businesses of any size, and give you a written summary of what we found before you commit to anything.
