Most businesses do not need a full-time IT department. They need someone reliable to answer when something breaks, and someone diligent enough that things break less often. Clearpine Technologies is an Abbotsford managed IT provider serving the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland, working under a written service agreement so expectations are contractual rather than hopeful.

What managed IT includes

  • Help desk. Phone and email support with defined response and resolution targets.
  • Endpoint management. Standard builds and patching on a schedule for workstations and laptops.
  • Microsoft 365 administration. User lifecycle, licensing, mailboxes and security hardening.
  • Security basics. Multi-factor authentication, email protection and a prioritized risk list.
  • Backup verification. Backups tested, not merely scheduled. See why backups quietly fail.
  • Monitoring and inventory. Alerts for disks, services and backups, plus a current asset and licence list.

What it costs

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users on a 1-year agreement. Microsoft 365 licences and after-hours support are billed separately. On-site visits are billed separately, but free if we cannot fix the problem remotely. Full details are on our services page.

Is managed IT right for you?

  • You have 5 or more staff and no dedicated IT person, or one person stretched thin
  • You are paying for repairs as they come and want predictable monthly costs
  • You want Microsoft 365, security and backup looked after by the same team

Managed IT: common questions

What is a managed service provider (MSP)?

A managed service provider takes ongoing responsibility for a business's IT: help desk, device management, patching, Microsoft 365 administration, security and backup. You pay a predictable monthly fee instead of paying for each problem as it happens.

How much do managed IT services cost in the Lower Mainland?

Clearpine managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users on a 1-year agreement. Microsoft 365 licences and after-hours support are billed separately. On-site visits are billed separately but are free if we cannot fix the problem remotely.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Abbotsford and provide on-site service across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland, including Chilliwack, Mission, Langley, Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby, New Westminster, Maple Ridge and North Vancouver, plus remote support across Canada.

Do you replace our in-house IT person?

Not necessarily. We can run IT end to end for businesses with no IT staff, or work alongside an internal person or team and take on the help desk, monitoring, patching or security work they do not have time for.

Is there a free way to start?

Yes. We offer a free, no-obligation IT assessment, on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford, with a written summary of findings.
