Managed IT Services for Lower Mainland & Fraser Valley Businesses

Managed service provider (MSP) · Remote-first from Abbotsford · $95/user/month

Clearpine Technologies is a managed service provider based in Abbotsford, BC. We run day-to-day IT for small and mid-sized businesses, clinics and law offices across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver for a flat monthly fee per user, so IT costs stay predictable and problems get fixed before they stop your team.

Most support is delivered remotely, which means the same service whether your office is in Chilliwack, Surrey or Vancouver. When something needs hands on site, we schedule a visit.

What’s included in managed IT

  • Help desk — your staff call or email us when something breaks, and we fix it remotely wherever possible.
  • Device and endpoint management — patching, updates and monitoring for workstations and laptops.
  • Microsoft 365 administration — accounts, email, Teams, SharePoint and permissions.
  • Cybersecurity — multi-factor authentication, firewall configuration and sensible security baselines for a small business.
  • Backup checks — backups that are tested, not just assumed. See why your backups probably aren’t working.
  • Co-managed IT — support for an existing in-house IT person, with after-hours coverage and project help.

Full detail is on our services page.

Managed IT pricing

Managed IT support is $95 per user per month, with a minimum of 5 users and a 1-year agreement. Support is delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately, but on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely.

For a 10-person office that is 10 × $95 = $950 per month before Microsoft 365 licences. Compare that with the cost and risk of a single full-time hire: see managed IT vs in-house IT for small business.

How to choose an MSP

Whichever provider you talk to, ask for these in writing:

  • The monthly price per user, and exactly what is billed on top (licences, after-hours, on-site).
  • The contract length and how to exit it.
  • Who you talk to when something breaks, and how you reach them.
  • How backups are tested and how often.
  • Whether you own your admin credentials and documentation.

Managed IT FAQ

What is a managed service provider (MSP)?

A managed service provider runs your day-to-day IT for a predictable monthly fee: help desk, device management, Microsoft 365 administration, security and backups, instead of you paying by the hour when something breaks.

How much does managed IT cost in the Lower Mainland?

Clearpine charges $95 per user per month (minimum 5 users, 1-year agreement), delivered remotely during business hours. Microsoft 365 licences, after-hours support and on-site visits are billed separately; on-site visits are free if the problem can’t be fixed remotely.

Where do you provide managed IT services?

Clearpine is headquartered in Abbotsford and supports businesses across the Fraser Valley and Metro Vancouver remotely, with scheduled on-site visits as needed.

Can you work alongside our existing IT person?

Yes. Co-managed IT adds after-hours coverage, project help and holiday cover to an in-house IT person or small team.

Is there a free way to see where our IT stands?

Yes. The free, no-obligation IT assessment can be done on site anywhere within 300 km of Abbotsford, or remotely.
