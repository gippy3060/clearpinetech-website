**Patterns worth copying, done honestly:**
1. One landing page per **service × city** pair, but only where the content is really different.
2. Pricing transparency ("from $X/user/month") and stated response times. Only publish these once the owner confirms real numbers.
3. A dedicated page for each high-value service: Microsoft 365, cybersecurity, backup/DR, co-managed IT, help desk, network/Wi-Fi.
4. "Free IT assessment" or "network audit" offers as the conversion point.
5. FAQ sections with FAQPage schema on service pages.

## Rules for this map
- Never mass-produce city-swapped pages. A city page needs local specifics (business districts, travel/onsite times from Abbotsford, local industries).
- Healthcare and law-firm vertical keywords belong to the *Lower Mainland MSP (doctors & lawyers)* routine. Home-user keywords belong to the *home remote support* routine. Link to their pages; don't duplicate them.
- Don't claim pricing, response times, certifications or partnerships until the owner confirms them. Mark these `<!-- TODO owner: ... -->`.
