Cybersecurity services for small businesses in the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley

Security for a small organization is not about buying an expensive product. It is a short list of controls, configured correctly and checked regularly. Clearpine Technologies sets them up, runs them and proves they work, from Abbotsford, for businesses across BC.

The controls that stop most small-business incidents

MFAFirewallsEndpoint protection SegmentationTested backupsAssessment

Most breaches we see in small environments come down to the same handful of gaps: an email account without multi-factor authentication, a former employee who still has access, a firewall left on factory settings, or a backup nobody has ever tried to restore. Closing those gaps is unglamorous work, and it is the work that matters.

What we do

  • Multi-factor authentication. Rolled out across email and remote access. It is the single highest-value control most organizations are still missing.
  • Firewall design and configuration. Perimeter security specified, deployed and documented, not left on factory defaults.
  • Network segmentation. Separating client or patient data, staff devices, guest Wi-Fi and equipment that cannot be patched, so one compromised device does not reach everything.
  • Endpoint protection. Managed antivirus and detection, monitored rather than installed and forgotten, with patching on a schedule.
  • Access control. Least-privilege accounts, administrator accounts separated from daily use, and offboarding that actually removes access on the day someone leaves.
  • Microsoft 365 hardening. Conditional access, mail flow rules and MFA enforcement included in our Microsoft 365 administration, not sold as an add-on.
  • Backup and recovery testing. Designed to a stated recovery objective and verified by performing restores. See why your backups are probably not working.
  • Security assessment. A documented review of your environment with findings ranked by risk and effort, and a plan you can act on with or without us.

Where to start if you have done nothing yet

If your business has no IT provider and no security plan, this is the order we usually work in. Each step is cheap compared with the problem it prevents.

  1. Turn on MFA for every email account, starting with the owner, the bookkeeper and anyone who approves payments.
  2. Find out what backups you have and restore one file to prove it works. Make sure at least one copy is off site and cannot be changed by a compromised account.
  3. List who has access to what, and remove accounts for people who have left.
  4. Put guests and personal devices on a separate Wi-Fi network from the computers that hold business data.
  5. Get every computer patched and protected, with endpoint protection someone is actually watching.
  6. Write down who to call if something goes wrong, including your insurer's breach line if you have cyber coverage.

Our free IT assessment checks all six and gives you the findings in writing.

Small business cybersecurity: common questions

What cybersecurity does a small business actually need?

A small number of controls, configured correctly and checked regularly: multi-factor authentication on email and remote access, managed endpoint protection, patching, least-privilege accounts with prompt offboarding, a properly configured firewall, a separate guest network, and backups that are tested by restoring them. Expensive products matter far less than getting these basics right.
